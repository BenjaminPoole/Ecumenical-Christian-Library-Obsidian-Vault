# Minor Historical Works

Bede's shorter historical and hagiographical works, together with the eyewitness letter on his death. Translations from J. A. Giles's *Minor Historical Works of Venerable Bede* (1843), except the death-letter (from Sellar's edition). These are converted from **uncorrected OCR** of the 1843 print, so occasional character errors and stray printed side-notes may remain in the text.

- [[The Life and Miracles of St Cuthbert|Life and Miracles of St Cuthbert]] — Bede's prose Life of St Cuthbert of Lindisfarne (46 chapters).
- [[Lives of the Holy Abbots of Wearmouth and Jarrow|Lives of the Holy Abbots of Wearmouth and Jarrow]] — The *Historia Abbatum* — lives of Benedict Biscop, Ceolfrid, and their successors.
- [[The Life of St Felix|Life of St Felix]] — Bede's prose paraphrase of Paulinus of Nola's verse life of St Felix of Nola.
- [[The Life of St Vedast|Life of St Vedast]] — Bede's abridgement of the life of St Vedast (Vaast) of Arras.
- [[The Letter of Bede to Abbot Albinus|Letter of Bede to Abbot Albinus]] — A short covering letter sending the completed *Ecclesiastical History* to Albinus.
- [[The Letter of Bede to Bishop Egbert|Letter of Bede to Bishop Egbert]] — Bede's last major work (734): a pastoral letter to Egbert of York on church reform.
- [[Of the Holy Places|Of the Holy Places]] — *De Locis Sanctis*, a description of Jerusalem and the holy sites, abridged from earlier writers.
- [[The Letter of Cuthbert on the Death of Bede|Letter of Cuthbert on the Death of Bede]] — Cuthbert's eyewitness account of Bede's death (735), including Bede's Death Song. By Bede's pupil, not Bede himself.
- [[The Seven Wonders of the World|The Seven Wonders of the World]] — *De septem mundi miraculis* — printed by Giles among Bede's works but generally regarded as **spurious** (pseudo-Bede).
