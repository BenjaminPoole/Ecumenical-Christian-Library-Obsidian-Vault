# Homilies

Bede preached and wrote fifty *Homilies on the Gospels*, widely read throughout the Middle Ages. Only one is available here: the modern critical translation of the full homiliary (Martin & Hurst, Cistercian Publications, 1991) remains in copyright, so the complete set cannot be included from public-domain sources.

- [[Homily on All Saints|Homily on All Saints]] — for the feast of All Saints (c. 710), translated by John Mason Neale (1906).
