# Ecclesiastical History of the English People

Bede's *Historia Ecclesiastica Gentis Anglorum*, completed in 731 — the foundational history of the English Church and people, and his most famous work. Translation: A. M. Sellar's 1907 revision of Giles (Project Gutenberg #38326). One note per chapter; scholarly footnotes are retained and renumbered per note.
