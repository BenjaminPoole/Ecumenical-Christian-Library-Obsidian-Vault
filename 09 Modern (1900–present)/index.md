# Modern (1900–present)

Christian writing from the twentieth century onward that is in the public domain.

## The Fundamentals

Ninety essays published between 1910 and 1915 by the Testimony Publishing Company of Chicago, funded by the brothers Lyman and Milton Stewart and edited in turn by A. C. Dixon, Louis Meyer, and R. A. Torrey. Written by leading conservative theologians of Britain and North America in defence of orthodox Protestant belief against the higher criticism and Darwinism, and mailed free of charge to pastors and missionaries across the English-speaking world. The work gave its name to the movement later called *fundamentalism*. Given here in the four-volume arrangement edited by Torrey for the Bible Institute of Los Angeles (1917).

Index: **[[The Fundamentals index|The Fundamentals: A Testimony to the Truth]]** *(91 notes)*

## G. K. Chesterton

English journalist, novelist and apologist (1874–1936), received into the Catholic Church in 1922. Given here: *Heretics* (1905), his criticism of the leading writers and ideas of his day, *Orthodoxy* (1908), his account of how he came to believe the Christian faith, *St. Francis of Assisi* (1923), his life of the founder of the Franciscans, *The Everlasting Man* (1925), his outline of human history and of Christ's place in it, written against H. G. Wells, and *St. Thomas Aquinas* (1933, also known as *The Dumb Ox*), his popular life of the Dominican philosopher and theologian.
