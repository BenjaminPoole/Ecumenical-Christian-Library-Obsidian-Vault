# G. K. Chesterton

Gilbert Keith Chesterton (1874–1936) was an English journalist, novelist, poet and Christian apologist, best known for his Father Brown stories, *Orthodoxy* (1908) and *The Everlasting Man* (1925). He was received into the Catholic Church in 1922.
