# SINNERS IN ZION TENDERLY WARNED:

### OR

## THE FEARFULNESS WHICH WILL HEREAFTER SURPRISE SINNERS IN ZION, REPRESENTED AND IMPROVED.

ISAIAH xxxiii. 14.

The sinners in Zion are afraid; fearfulness hath surprised the hypocrites. Who among us shall dwell with the devouring fire? who among us shall dwell with everlasting burnings? [^181]

[^181]: The substance of two posthumous discourses, dated Dec. 1740
