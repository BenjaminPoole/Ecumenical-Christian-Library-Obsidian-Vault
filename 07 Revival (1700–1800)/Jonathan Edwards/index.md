# The Works of Jonathan Edwards

The two-volume *Works of Jonathan Edwards* (Banner of Truth edition, revised and corrected by Edward Hickman, 1834), from the [CCEL](https://ccel.org/ccel/edwards) text. Volume 1 opens with the *Memoirs of Jonathan Edwards* by Sereno E. Dwight, followed by the major treatises; Volume 2 contains the sermons, the shorter treatises, the Life and Diary of David Brainerd, and the biblical notes. Public domain.
