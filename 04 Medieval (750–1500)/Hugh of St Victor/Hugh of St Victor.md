# Hugh of St Victor

Hugh of St Victor (c. 1096–1141), master of the abbey school of St Victor in Paris, the most influential teacher of the twelfth century after Anselm. He held that every created thing is a sign, and that the liberal arts are the road to Scripture rather than a distraction from it; his pupils carried that method through the schools of Paris.
