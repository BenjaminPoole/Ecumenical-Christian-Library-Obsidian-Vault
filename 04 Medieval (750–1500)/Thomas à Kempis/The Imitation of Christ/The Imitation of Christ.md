# The Imitation of Christ

*Thomas à Kempis* — translated by William Benham (1874), from [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1653).

The most widely read Christian book after the Bible, written in the houses of the *Devotio Moderna* and circulating anonymously from about 1420. Four books: admonitions for the spiritual life, admonitions concerning inward things, of inward consolation, and of the sacrament — the last turning from counsel into direct address between Christ and the disciple.
