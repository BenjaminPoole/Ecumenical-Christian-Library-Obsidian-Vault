# Medieval Theology

Latin theology between the Carolingian renewal and the eve of the Reformation, roughly 750 to 1500. The section holds one author at present — Thomas Aquinas, in his two great summaries — and 1,085 notes.

## Thomas Aquinas

St Thomas Aquinas (c. 1225–1274), Dominican friar, priest, and Doctor of the Church. His two theological summaries are here in public-domain or freely available English translations.

Author index: **[[Thomas Aquinas|Thomas Aquinas]]**

- **[[Summa Theologiae|Summa Theologiae]]** — his masterwork (1265–1274), in five Parts: 611 Questions and some 2,600 Articles. Translated by the Fathers of the English Dominican Province (1920); public domain, from [New Advent](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/). *(617 notes)*
- **[[Summa Contra Gentiles|Summa Contra Gentiles]]** — the earlier apologetic summa (c. 1259–1265), in four Books and 462 chapters. Hanover House translation (1955–57), edited by Joseph Kenny O.P., from [isidore.co](https://isidore.co/aquinas/ContraGentiles.htm). *(467 notes)*
