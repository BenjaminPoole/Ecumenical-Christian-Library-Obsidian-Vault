# Julian of Norwich

Julian of Norwich (1343–c. 1416), an anchoress enclosed beside the church of St Julian in Norwich. On 8 May 1373, expecting to die, she received sixteen *shewings*, and spent some twenty years thinking about what they meant. Hers is the earliest surviving book in English known to be written by a woman.

## Works (1)

- **[[Revelations of Divine Love|Revelations of Divine Love]]** — The sixteen *shewings* granted to an anchoress of Norwich on 8 May 1373, as she lay expecting to die, and the twenty years of meditation she gave them: the hazelnut that is all that is made, the motherhood of God, and the answer she was given about sin — *that all shall be well, and all shall be well, and all manner of thing shall be well*.
