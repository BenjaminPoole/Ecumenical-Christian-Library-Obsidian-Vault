# Ratramnus of Corbie

Ratramnus, monk of Corbie (d. c. 870), wrote at the request of Charles the Bald against the eucharistic teaching of his own abbot, Paschasius Radbertus. His treatise was condemned in 1050, printed by the reformers as *The Boke of Barthram*, and used by Ridley and Cranmer — which is why a ninth-century Carolingian monk matters to English church history.

## Works (1)

- **[[On the Body and Blood of the Lord|On the Body and Blood of the Lord]]** — Written at the request of Charles the Bald, and against his own abbot Paschasius Radbertus: the body in the sacrament is Christ's body *in figura*, not the same flesh born of Mary in visible form. Condemned in 1050, printed by the reformers as *The Boke of Barthram*, and read by Ridley and Cranmer — which is why the Latin original is printed after the English in this edition, and why only the English is taken here.
