# The Life of Christ

*Bonaventure* — *The Life of Christ*, translated and edited by the Rev. W. H. Hutchings (London: Rivingtons, 1888; first published 1881), read from the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/TheLifeOfChristBonaventure).

Meditations on the life of Christ from the Incarnation to the Ascension, long attributed to Bonaventure and among the most widely copied devotional books of the later Middle Ages. Hutchings translates the shorter recension: the author’s Preface and a hundred chapters. The translator’s own Introduction is not included.
