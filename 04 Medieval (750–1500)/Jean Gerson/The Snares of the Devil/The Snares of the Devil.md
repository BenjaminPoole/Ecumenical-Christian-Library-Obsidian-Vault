# The Snares of the Devil

*Jean Gerson* — translated by “Beta” (London: Thomas Richardson and Son, 1883), from [CCEL](https://ccel.org/ccel/gerson/snares).

A short treatise of practical discernment by the chancellor of Paris — the temptations that beset the soul at each stage of the spiritual life, and the remedies against them.
