# Life of St Malachy of Armagh

*Bernard of Clairvaux* — translated by H. J. Lawlor (London: SPCK, 1920), from [CCEL](https://ccel.org/ccel/bernard/st_malachy).

Bernard's life of his friend Malachy, archbishop of Armagh, who died at Clairvaux in 1148 — the chief narrative source for the twelfth-century reform of the Irish church.
