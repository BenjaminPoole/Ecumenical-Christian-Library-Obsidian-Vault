# Cur Deus Homo

*Anselm of Canterbury* — translated by James Gardiner Vose (*Bibliotheca Sacra*, 1854–55), printed with the Deane volume, from [CCEL](https://ccel.org/ccel/anselm/basic_works).

*Why God Became Man* (1094–98), a dialogue with the monk Boso and the classic statement of the satisfaction theory of the atonement: sin is an infinite dishonour to God which man owes and cannot pay, and God alone can pay but does not owe — so the one who makes satisfaction must be both God and man.
