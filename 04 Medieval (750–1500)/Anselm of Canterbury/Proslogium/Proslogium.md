# Proslogium

*Anselm of Canterbury* — translated by Sidney Norton Deane (Open Court, 1903; reprint 1926), from [CCEL](https://ccel.org/ccel/anselm/basic_works).

Written 1077–78 as *Fides quaerens intellectum*, “faith seeking understanding.” It opens in prayer and arrives, in Chapters 2–4, at the argument that God is *that than which nothing greater can be conceived* — the ontological argument, which has been in dispute ever since. Gaunilo's objection and Anselm's reply are in [[In Behalf of the Fool|In Behalf of the Fool]].
