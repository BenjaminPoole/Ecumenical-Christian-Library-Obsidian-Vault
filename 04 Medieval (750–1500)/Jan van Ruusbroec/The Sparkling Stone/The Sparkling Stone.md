# The Sparkling Stone

*Jan van Ruusbroec* — translated from the Flemish by C. A. Wynschenk Dom, edited by Evelyn Underhill (London: J. M. Dent, 1916), from [CCEL](https://ccel.org/ccel/ruysbroeck/adornment).

A shorter treatise on the white stone of Revelation 2:17, and on the difference between the faithful servant, the secret friend, and the hidden son of God.
