# Chapter 4. Order of Procedure

*Book Two — Creation, Chapter 4* — Summa Contra Gentiles

[1] We shall treat of these matters in the following order: first, the bringing forth of things into being [6-38]; second, their distinction [39-45]; third, the nature of these same things, brought forth and distinct from one another, so far as it is relevant to the truth of the faith [46-101].
