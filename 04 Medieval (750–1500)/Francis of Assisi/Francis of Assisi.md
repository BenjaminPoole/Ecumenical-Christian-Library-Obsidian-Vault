# Francis of Assisi

Francis of Assisi (c. 1181–1226), founder of the Friars Minor, who took the Gospel as his Rule and left a small body of writings: counsels, rules, letters, prayers and the *Canticle of the Sun*. This is the whole collection in Paschal Robinson’s English translation (Philadelphia, 1906), made from the critical Quaracchi edition of 1904, with his introductions and appendix. The *Little Flowers of St. Francis’ (Fioretti) are not included: they were compiled in the fourteenth century and are not by Francis.

## Works (20)

- **[[Francis of Assisi/Admonitions|Admonitions]]** — The twenty-eight Words of Admonition: short counsels on obedience, poverty, humility, charity and the Blessed Sacrament, with Robinson’s note on the manuscripts.
- **[[Francis of Assisi/Salutation of the Virtues|Salutation of the Virtues]]** — The salutation to Lady Wisdom, Holy Poverty, Charity and the other virtues, which confound the vices opposed to them.
- **[[Francis of Assisi/On Reverence for the Lord's Body|On Reverence for the Lord's Body]]** — Francis’ instruction to all clerics on the care owed to the Body and Blood of the Lord, the chalices and corporals, and the written Names of God.
- **[[Francis of Assisi/First Rule of the Friars Minor|First Rule of the Friars Minor]]** — The longer, unsealed Rule of the brothers (1209–1221), in twenty-three chapters, which closes with the great prayer and thanksgiving of chapter 23.
- **[[Francis of Assisi/Second Rule of the Friars Minor|Second Rule of the Friars Minor]]** — The shorter Rule of twelve chapters, confirmed by Pope Honorius III on 29 November 1223.
- **[[Francis of Assisi/Fragments from the Rule of the Poor Clares|Fragments from the Rule of the Poor Clares]]** — The two fragments of Francis’ writing to St. Clare and her sisters, preserved in the sixth chapter of her Rule: the form of life and the last wish.
- **[[Francis of Assisi/Testament|Testament]]** — Francis’ Testament of 1226: his conversion among the lepers, his reverence for priests, the Rule and the brothers’ labour and poverty.
- **[[Francis of Assisi/Regulation for Hermitages|Regulation for Hermitages]]** — The short rule for friars living in hermitages, three or four together, as mothers and sons, in the lives of Martha and Mary.
- **[[Francis of Assisi/Letter to All the Faithful|Letter to All the Faithful]]** — The letter to all Christians: penance, the Eucharist, the love of neighbour, and the death of the miser.
- **[[Francis of Assisi/Letter to All the Friars|Letter to All the Friars]]** — The letter to the General Chapter and all the friars: reverence for the Eucharist and the priesthood, and Francis’ confession and counsel.
- **[[Francis of Assisi/Letter to a Certain Minister|Letter to a Certain Minister]]** — Counsel to a minister burdened by the offences of his brothers: love them, forgive without limit, and bear it patiently.
- **[[Francis of Assisi/Letter to the Rulers of the People|Letter to the Rulers of the People]]** — The letter to the podestàs, consuls and judges of the world, bidding them remember the day of death and honour the Lord’s Body and Blood.
- **[[Francis of Assisi/Letter to All the Custodes|Letter to All the Custodes]]** — The letter to the custodes of the Friars Minor, on the honour due to the Eucharist and the Names of the Lord, and on preaching penance.
- **[[Francis of Assisi/Letter to Brother Leo|Letter to Brother Leo]]** — The autograph note to Brother Leo, preserved at Spoleto: “I say to thee, Yes, my son, and as a mother.”
- **[[Francis of Assisi/Paraphrase of the Lord's Prayer and Praises|Paraphrase of the Lord’s Prayer and Praises]]** — Francis’ line-by-line paraphrase of the Our Father and the Praises (“Holy, Holy, Holy”), with the closing prayer.
- **[[Francis of Assisi/Salutation of the Blessed Virgin|Salutation of the Blessed Virgin]]** — The salutation to Mary as palace, tabernacle, house, garment and handmaid of the Lord.
- **[[Francis of Assisi/Prayer to Obtain Divine Love|Prayer to Obtain Divine Love]]** — The short prayer that the fiery and sweet strength of Christ’s love may absorb the soul.
- **[[Francis of Assisi/Praises of God and Blessing for Brother Leo|Praises of God and Blessing for Brother Leo]]** — The Praises of God and the Blessing for Brother Leo, written on La Verna in 1224 on the parchment given to Leo.
- **[[Francis of Assisi/Canticle of the Sun|Canticle of the Sun]]** — The Canticle of Brother Sun, or the Praises of the Creatures, composed in 1225 at San Damiano.
- **[[Francis of Assisi/Office of the Passion|Office of the Passion]]** — The Psalms and antiphons which Francis arranged to recall the Passion of the Lord, for every hour of the liturgical day.
