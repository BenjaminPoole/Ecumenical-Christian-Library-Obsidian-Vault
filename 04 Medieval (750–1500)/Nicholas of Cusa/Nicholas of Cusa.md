# Nicholas of Cusa

Nicholas of Cusa (1401–1464), canon lawyer at the Council of Basel, papal legate to Constantinople, cardinal and bishop of Brixen — and the most original speculative mind of the fifteenth century. He argued that God is reached not by reasoning but by *learned ignorance*, at the point where opposites coincide and the intellect gives out.

## Works (1)

- **[[The Vision of God|The Vision of God]]** — Written in 1453 for the monks of Tegernsee, who had asked him to explain mystical theology. Cusa sends them a painting whose eyes follow every viewer at once, and builds from it an account of a God whose seeing is His creating, approached through the *coincidence of opposites* that reason cannot cross.
