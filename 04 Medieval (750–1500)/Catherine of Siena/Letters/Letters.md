# Letters

*Catherine of Siena* — translated and edited by Vida D. Scudder (London: J. M. Dent, 1905), from [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/7403).

Letters to popes, kings, mercenaries, prisoners, prostitutes and her own family, written by a dyer's daughter who could not write and dictated them — including the letters urging Gregory XI to leave Avignon for Rome. Scudder groups them by correspondent and prefaces each group.
