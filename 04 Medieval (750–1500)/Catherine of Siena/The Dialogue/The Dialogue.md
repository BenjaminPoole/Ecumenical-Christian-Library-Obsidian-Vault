# The Dialogue

*Catherine of Siena* — translated by Algar Thorold, from [CCEL](https://ccel.org/ccel/catherine/dialog).

Dictated in 1378, much of it in ecstasy: a dialogue between the Eternal Father and a soul, on discretion, prayer, obedience, and the bridge that is Christ, by which the soul crosses the flood.
