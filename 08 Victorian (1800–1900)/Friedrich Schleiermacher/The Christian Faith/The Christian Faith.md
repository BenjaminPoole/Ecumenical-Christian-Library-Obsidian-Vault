# The Christian Faith

*Friedrich Schleiermacher* — *The Christian Faith*, translated from the second German edition (1830–1831) and edited by H. R. Mackintosh and J. S. Stewart (Edinburgh: T. & T. Clark, 1928), transcribed from the page images of the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/christianfaith0000schl_i5l0).

The great systematic theology of the father of modern liberal theology, in which doctrine is the church's account of the Christian's God-consciousness, "the feeling of absolute dependence", as Christ has shaped it. After an introduction on the church, on Christianity and on the method of dogmatics, the first part develops the religious self-consciousness that every Christian experience presupposes (creation, preservation, and the divine attributes of eternity, omnipresence, omnipotence and omniscience); the second part sets out its two sides, the consciousness of sin (original and actual sin, the holiness and justice of God) and the consciousness of grace (the person and work of Christ, regeneration, sanctification, election, the church, the means of grace and the last things); and a conclusion treats the divine Trinity. The book's indexes at the end are left out.

**Copyright.** This translation is in the public domain in the United States, where works published in 1928 passed out of copyright in 2024. J. S. Stewart died in 1990, so outside the United States it remains in copyright until 2060.
