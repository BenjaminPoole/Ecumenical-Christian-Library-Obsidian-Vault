# Keep Thy Solemn Feasts

*Abraham Kuyper* — *Keep Thy Solemn Feasts: Meditations*, translated from the Dutch by John Hendrik de Vries (Grand Rapids: Wm. B. Eerdmans, 1928), transcribed from the page images of the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/keepthysolemnfea00kuyp). Published in 1928, it is in the public domain in the United States (the 95-year term ended in 2023); it may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Thirty-six meditations for the great days of the Christian year (Kuyper's foreword is dated The Hague, 1 December 1903): nine for Christmas, nine for Easter, three for Ascension, six for Pentecost and nine for the Old and New Year. With a foreword by Kuyper and a translator's preface on his life and work.
