# Encyclopedia of Sacred Theology

*Abraham Kuyper* — *Encyclopedia of Sacred Theology: Its Principles*, translated from the Dutch by J. Hendrik de Vries, with an introductory note by Benjamin B. Warfield (New York: Charles Scribner's Sons, 1898), transcribed from the page images of the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/encyclopediaofsa00kuyp), Warfield's own copy, presented to the library of Princeton Theological Seminary.

The English version of the general part of Kuyper's three-volume *Encyclopaedie der Heilige Godgeleerdheid* (1894). In three divisions Kuyper traces the name and idea of encyclopedia; sets out the organism of science, how sin has impaired it, and why regeneration produces two kinds of people and so two kinds of science; and then treats theology itself — its conception, its principium in the self-revelation of God and the Holy Scripture, its method, its organism and its history. The index of proper names at the end of the book is left out.
