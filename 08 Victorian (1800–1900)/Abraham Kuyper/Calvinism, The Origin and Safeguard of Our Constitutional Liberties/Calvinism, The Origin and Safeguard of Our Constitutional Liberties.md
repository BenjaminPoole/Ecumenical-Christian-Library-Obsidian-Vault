# Calvinism, The Origin and Safeguard of Our Constitutional Liberties

*Abraham Kuyper* — *Calvinism: The Origin and Safeguard of Our Constitutional Liberties*, translated from the Dutch by J. Hendrik de Vries and printed in the *Bibliotheca Sacra* for October 1895 (pp. 645–674; this is the offprint), transcribed from the page images of the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/calvinismorigins00kuyp). Published in 1895, it is in the public domain.

The second of Kuyper's articles on the Reformed roots of political liberty. It goes back from the Calvinistic troubles in England to the Huguenots of France, and compares Coligny's plans and the Huguenot constitution with the Independents and, in outline, the American Constitution; it asks why the Calvinist principle of the separation of church and state was not carried through, and ends with Kuyper's own programme for the Netherlands.
