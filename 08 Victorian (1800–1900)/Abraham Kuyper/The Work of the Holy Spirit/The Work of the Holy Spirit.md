# The Work of the Holy Spirit

*Abraham Kuyper* — translated from the Dutch by Henri De Vries, with an introduction by Benjamin B. Warfield (New York: Funk & Wagnalls, 1900), from [CCEL](https://ccel.org/ccel/kuyper/holy_spirit).

*Het Werk van den Heiligen Geest* (1888): the work of the Spirit in creation and re-creation, in Scripture, the incarnation and the Church, and then in the individual — regeneration, calling, justification, faith, sanctification, love and prayer.
