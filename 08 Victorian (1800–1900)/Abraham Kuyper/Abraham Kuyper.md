# Abraham Kuyper

Abraham Kuyper (1837–1920), pastor, journalist, founder of the Free University of Amsterdam (1880) and of the Anti-Revolutionary Party, leader of the secession from the Dutch Reformed Church in 1886, and Prime Minister of the Netherlands from 1901 to 1905. The founder of Neo-Calvinism: "there is not a square inch in the whole domain of our human existence over which Christ, who is Sovereign over all, does not cry: *Mine!*" He edited the daily *De Standaard* and the weekly *De Heraut*, in which most of his devotional and theological books first appeared.

## Works (3)

- **[[Lectures on Calvinism|Lectures on Calvinism]]** — The L. P. Stone Lectures given at Princeton Theological Seminary in October 1898: Calvinism not as a church's confession but as a life-system, and its bearing on religion, politics, science and art, and on the future. Kuyper's account of sphere sovereignty is in the third lecture, and his doctrine of common grace in the fourth. (6 notes)
- **[[The Work of the Holy Spirit|The Work of the Holy Spirit]]** — *Het Werk van den Heiligen Geest* (1888): the work of the Spirit in creation and re-creation, in Scripture, the incarnation and the Church, and then in the individual — regeneration, calling, justification, faith, sanctification, love and prayer. (126 notes)
- **[[To Be Near Unto God|To Be Near Unto God]]** — *Nabij God te zijn*: one hundred and ten meditations, written week by week for *De Heraut*, on Psalm 73:28 — "It is good for me to draw near to God." (110 notes)
