# Translator's Dedication

*THAT the message of strength and comfort, which this volume brings to the sick-room may continue its mission in the new, as it does in its original, dress, is the prayer wherewith the Translator offers it to the English-speaking Christian world.*

*Walpole, Mass., July 28, 1929.*
