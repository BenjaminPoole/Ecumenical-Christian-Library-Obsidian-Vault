# The Antithesis between Symbolism and Revelation

*Abraham Kuyper* — *The Antithesis between Symbolism and Revelation: Lecture Delivered before the Historical Presbyterian Society in Philadelphia, Pa.* (Amsterdam–Pretoria: Höveker & Wormser; Edinburgh: T. & T. Clark; London: Simpkin, Marshall, Hamilton, Kent, [1899]), from [sources.neocalvinism.org](https://sources.neocalvinism.org/k-27728-Abraham+Kuyper.+The+Antithesis+between+Symbolism+and+Revelation), checked against the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/antithesisbetwee00kuyp).

A lecture Kuyper gave to the Historical Presbyterian Society in Philadelphia, on the Stone Lectures trip of 1898: the ritualism that was drawing the children of English non-conformists back to the episcopalian services and now threatened Calvinist church life. Symbolism, he argues, replaces revelation and falls back from conscious to unconscious religion, while Calvinism puts the Word first and allows only such forms of worship as echo it; he closes with a test for telling the harmful influence of the symbolical from a right love of liturgical reform. The Dutch version appeared in *De Heraut* in December 1898.
