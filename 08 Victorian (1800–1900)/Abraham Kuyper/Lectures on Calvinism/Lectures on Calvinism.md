# Lectures on Calvinism

*Abraham Kuyper* — *Calvinism: Six Stone-Lectures* (Amsterdam-Pretoria: Höveker & Wormser; New York: Fleming H. Revell, 1899), read again from the page images of the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/calvinismsixstonny00kuyp).

The L. P. Stone Lectures given at Princeton Theological Seminary in October 1898: Calvinism not as a church's confession but as a life-system, and its bearing on religion, politics, science and art, and on the future. Kuyper's account of sphere sovereignty is in the third lecture, and his doctrine of common grace in the fourth.
