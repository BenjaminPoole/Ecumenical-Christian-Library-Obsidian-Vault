# The Crowd is Untruth

*Søren Kierkegaard* — translated by Charles K. Bellinger, who placed his translation in the public domain, from [CCEL](https://ccel.org/ccel/kierkegaard/untruth).

Written in 1847 to go with the dedication of *Upbuilding Discourses in Various Spirits* to "that single individual", and published after his death with *The Point of View for My Work as an Author*: "the crowd is untruth".
