# Selections from the Writings of Kierkegaard

*Søren Kierkegaard* — translated by Lee M. Hollander (Austin: University of Texas, *Bulletin* no. 2326, 1923), from [CCEL](https://ccel.org/ccel/kierkegaard/selections).

The first book of Kierkegaard in English: Hollander's introduction, and passages from *Either/Or* (the Diapsalmata), *Stages on Life's Way* (The Banquet), *Fear and Trembling*, *Training in Christianity* (as "Preparation for a Christian Life") and *The Instant*.
