# Dedication and Advertisement

## Dedication

REVERENDO PATRI

ANTONIO BRESCIANI

E SOCIETATE IESV

COLLEGII VRBANI RECTORI

VIRO LECTISSIMO

NOBIS HOSPITIBVS SVIS ET LINGVA PÆNE BARBARIS

ACCEPTISSIMO

HOC SERENISSIMORVM DIERVM

SVB EIVS TVTELA ACTORVM

QVALECVNQVE MONVMENTVM

COMMENDATVM DICATVMQVE

VELIMVS.

## Advertisement
