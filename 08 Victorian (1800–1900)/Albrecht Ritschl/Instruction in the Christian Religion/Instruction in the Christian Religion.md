# Instruction in the Christian Religion

*Albrecht Ritschl* — *Instruction in the Christian Religion*, translated by Alice Mead Swing and printed in Albert Temple Swing, *The Theology of Albrecht Ritschl* (New York: Longmans, Green, and Co., 1901), transcribed from the page images of the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/theologyofritsch00swinuoft).

Ritschl's *Unterricht in der christlichen Religion* (first published 1875; this translation follows the fourth German edition), a compact set of numbered paragraphs, with notes, that he wrote as a manual of his own theology. After an introduction on the standpoint of the Christian community, it sets out the doctrine of the kingdom of God, the doctrine of reconciliation through Christ, the doctrine of the Christian life, and the doctrine of public worship. Swing's own study of Ritschl that precedes it in the volume, and the index, are left out.
