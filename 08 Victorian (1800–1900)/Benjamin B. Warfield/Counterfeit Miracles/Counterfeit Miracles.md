# Counterfeit Miracles

*Benjamin B. Warfield* — *Counterfeit Miracles* (New York: Charles Scribner's Sons, 1918), read again from the page images of the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/counterfeitmirac00warf). The notes to each lecture follow the lectures, as the book prints them. They are the OCR of the page images: their numbering has been repaired where the sequence allowed, a few short notes still sit inside the note before them, and the figures that refer to them in the text are not reproduced. Page and volume numbers in the notes are set in old-style figures that OCR misreads, so check them against the scan before citing them.

The Thomas Smyth Lectures for 1917–1918, delivered at Columbia Theological Seminary, South Carolina. Warfield argues that the miraculous gifts of the apostolic age were the credentials of the apostles and ceased with them, and then examines the claims to miracle that followed: the marvels of the Fathers and the Middle Ages, the miracles of Lourdes and the Roman Church, the tongues and prophecies of Edward Irving's congregation, the faith-healing movement, and the mind-cure of Christian Science.
