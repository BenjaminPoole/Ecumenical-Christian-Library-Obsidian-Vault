# Calvin's Doctrine of God

*Benjamin B. Warfield* — *Calvin's Doctrine of God* (Princeton: Princeton Theological Review Association, 1909; from *The Princeton Theological Review*, vii. 1909), from the [Monergism](https://www.monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/warfield/warfield_doctrinegod.html) text, marked up by Lance George Marshall. It has not been read against the print; see "Works taken from an e-text" in `docs/transcription.md`. The [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/calvinsdoctrineo00warf) is the print.

Warfield gathers Calvin's doctrine of God from the *Institutes*, which have no formal chapter on it: the sense of deity and the knowledge of God, God's nature and attributes, His majesty and fatherhood, the jealousy for His honour that Calvin's war on idolatry shows, with the incidental matters of images, the pictorial arts and preaching, and Calvin's doctrine of the Trinity. Calvin, he argues, is no cold logician but a man whose theology is religion, a reverent sobriety before the God he knows.
