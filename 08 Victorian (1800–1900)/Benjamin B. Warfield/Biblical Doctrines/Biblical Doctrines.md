# Biblical Doctrines

*Benjamin B. Warfield* — *Biblical Doctrines*, volume II of his works (New York: Oxford University Press, 1929), from the [Monergism](https://www.monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/warfield/Biblical%20Doctrines-Warfield.pdf) PDF (2026), whose text was scanned, proofread and marked up by Lance George Marshall. Monergism's title page is left out. It has not been read against the print: the figures standing for footnotes are lost in some chapters, which keep their notes as a list at the end, and several articles give their scripture references in a modern form; see "Works taken from an e-text" in `docs/transcription.md`. The [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/biblicaldoctrinewarf) is the print. Published in 1929, it is in the public domain in the United States; the committee's prefatory note and the other editorial matter of the volume may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Sixteen articles on the doctrines of the Bible, mostly written for Hastings's dictionaries and the *International Standard Bible Encyclopaedia*: predestination, the foresight of Jesus, the Spirit of God in the Old Testament, the biblical doctrine of the Trinity, the person of Christ, "God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ", the Christ that Paul preached, Jesus' own account of His mission, the New Testament terms for redemption, Christ our sacrifice, renewal, faith, the terminology of love, the prophecies of St. Paul, and the millennium and the Apocalypse.
