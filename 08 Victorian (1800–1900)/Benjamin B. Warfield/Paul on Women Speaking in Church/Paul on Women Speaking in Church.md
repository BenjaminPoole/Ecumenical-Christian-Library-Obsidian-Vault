# Paul on Women Speaking in Church

*Benjamin B. Warfield* — *Paul on Women Speaking in Church* (from *The Presbyterian*, 30 October 1919), from the [Trinity Foundation](https://www.trinityfoundation.org/journal.php?id=236) text (*The Trinity Review*, no. 47a). The Foundation's editor's note, download links and copyright notice are left out. The text has not been read in full against the print; see "Works taken from an e-text" in `docs/transcription.md`. The [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/paulonwomenspeak00warf) is the print.

A reply to a friend who asked what the Greek words *laleo* and *lego* mean in 1 Corinthians 14:33-39, and whether verse 34 forbids all women to speak or preach in church. Warfield shows that *laleo* is simply "to talk" and carries nothing disparaging, then argues from 1 Corinthians 14 and 1 Timothy 2 that the prohibition is precise, absolute and all-inclusive, grounded in the order of creation and the fall, and that the difference between Paul and the feminist movement lies in their view of the race as families or as individuals.
