# The Acts and Pastoral Epistles (Introductions and Notes)

*Benjamin B. Warfield* (editor) — the introductions and notes he wrote for *The Acts and Pastoral Epistles* (The Temple Bible, London: J. M. Dent; Philadelphia: J. B. Lippincott, 1902), transcribed from the page images of the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/actsandpastoral00warfgoog).

The volume prints the Authorised Version of Acts, 1 and 2 Timothy, Titus and Philemon, with marginal catch-words only. That biblical text, the series' preliminary note, its tables of ancient history and its list of biblical references in English literature are not Warfield's and are left out here. What follows is his own: the introductions to Acts, to the Pastoral Epistles and to Philemon, and his notes on the renderings of the text.
