# The Development of the Doctrine of Infant Salvation

*Benjamin B. Warfield* — *The Development of the Doctrine of Infant Salvation* (New York: The Christian Literature Company, 1891), from the [Monergism](https://www.monergism.com/development-doctrine-infant-salvation) text. It has not been read against the print; see "Works taken from an e-text" in `docs/transcription.md`. The footnote figures sit in the e-text's running text and are set here as footnotes, matched to the notes in order.

Warfield traces how the doctrine of the salvation of infants dying in infancy has been held in every age of the Church and yet corrected: the Fathers' necessity of baptism and Augustine's mildest damnation, the medieval mitigation, the teaching of Rome, the Lutheran, Anglican and Reformed doctrines from Zwingli to Dort and Westminster, the "ethical" tendencies of later Protestantism, and the doctrinal development which makes the salvation of the infant rest on God's electing grace.
