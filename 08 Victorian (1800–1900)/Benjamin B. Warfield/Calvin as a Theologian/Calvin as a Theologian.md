# Calvin as a Theologian

*Benjamin B. Warfield* — *Calvin as a Theologian*, the address given in 1909, the four-hundredth anniversary of Calvin's birth, from the [Monergism](https://www.monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/warfield/warfield_calvintheologian.html) text, marked up by Lance George Marshall. It has not been read against the print; see "Works taken from an e-text" in `docs/transcription.md`.

An address on what manner of theologian Calvin was. Warfield asks the hearer to put aside the picture of Calvin as a speculative and logical audacity and shows instead his positiveness, his dependence on Scripture and on the Spirit's witness, and his place as the systematiser of the Reformation, the theologian of the Holy Spirit.
