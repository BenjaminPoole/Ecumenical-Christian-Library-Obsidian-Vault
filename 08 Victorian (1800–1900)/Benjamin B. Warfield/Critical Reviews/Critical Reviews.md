# Critical Reviews

*Benjamin B. Warfield* — *Critical Reviews*, volume X (and last) of his works (New York: Oxford University Press, 1932), from the [Monergism](https://www.monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/warfield/Volume%2010_Critical%20Reviews_Warfield.pdf) edition (2026; the EPUB, whose accented letters are sound, unlike the PDF's text layer), whose text was scanned, proofread and marked up by Lance George Marshall. Monergism's title page is left out; each review's note says where it first appeared. It has not been read against the print; see "Works taken from an e-text" in `docs/transcription.md`. The [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/criticalreviews0000warf) is the print. Published in 1932, it is in the public domain in the United States (the user confirmed this for the volumes of 1931-32); the committee's prefatory note and the other editorial matter of the volume may still be in copyright elsewhere.

A selection of forty-six of Warfield's reviews from *The Presbyterian and Reformed Review* and *The Princeton Theological Review*, 1890s-1920s, each headed by the book reviewed, with an index of authors. They range over the Bible and its text (Burgon, Kenyon, Dods, Seeberg), the dictionaries and encyclopaedias (Hastings, Cheyne), the person and work of Christ and the atonement (Denney, Sabatier, Sanday, Mackintosh, Weiss, Heitmüller), Calvin and Augustine (Doumergue, McCabe), mysticism (Underhill, Fresenius, Fleming) and the philosophy of religion (McTaggart, Bousset, Otto).
