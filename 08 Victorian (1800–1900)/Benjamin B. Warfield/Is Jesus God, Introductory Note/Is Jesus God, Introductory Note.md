# Introductory Note to Is Jesus God?

*Benjamin B. Warfield* — the *Introductory Note* to *Is Jesus God? Or, Jesus Christ the Lord of Glory: An Argument* (New York: Doran, 1912), pp. vii-viii, transcribed from the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/isjesusgodargum00warf). This is the only part of the book by Warfield; the essays that follow are by Princeton Seminary students (Kuiper, Joubert, Meeter, Roos, Richardson, Motter, Nicol, Hoeksema and Bicknell) and are not his, so they are not in the library under his name.

Warfield explains how the little volume arose: the members of a Princeton Seminary class, in the Centennial Session of 1911-1912, answered a series of six questions leading from what the Church teaches of the Deity of Christ to the conclusion that Christ is God. He says why one essay is printed on each of the earlier questions and two on each of the later, and commends the work of these young men to a candid reading.
