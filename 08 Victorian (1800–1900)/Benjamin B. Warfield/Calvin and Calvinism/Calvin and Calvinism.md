# Calvin and Calvinism

*Benjamin B. Warfield* — *Calvin and Calvinism*, volume V of his works (New York: Oxford University Press, 1931), from the [Monergism](https://www.monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/warfield/Vol%205_Calvin%20&%20Calvinism_Warfield.pdf) edition (2026; the EPUB, which keeps the footnote figures that the PDF's text lacks), whose text was scanned, proofread and marked up by Lance George Marshall. Monergism's title page and the illustrations it adds to chapter 1 are left out; the committee's prefatory note is the print's. Every footnote is set as a footnote. It has not been read against the print in full; see "Works taken from an e-text" in `docs/transcription.md`. The [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/calvincalvinism0000benj) is the print. Published in 1931, it is in the public domain in the United States (the user confirmed this for the volumes of 1931-32); the committee's prefatory note and the other editorial matter of the volume may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Warfield's articles on Calvin and Calvinism, the fifth volume of his works: the sketch of Calvin's life and work (*John Calvin: The Man and his Work*), the three long studies of the *Institutes* (the knowledge of God, the doctrine of God, the doctrine of the creation), the article *Calvinism* from the *New Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia*, and the history of the *Institutes* from the Memorial Edition. Chapters 3 and 4 are the book's text of two essays the library already has from the *Princeton Theological Review*, [[Calvin's Doctrine of the Knowledge of God]] and [[Calvin's Doctrine of God]] (the wording agrees with those notes in about 97 words in 100; the book's own wording is the one here). [[Calvin as a Theologian]], the 1909 address, is not in this volume.
