# Christology and Criticism

*Benjamin B. Warfield* — *Christology and Criticism*, volume III of his works (New York and London: Oxford University Press, 1929), from the [Monergism](https://monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/warfield/ChristologyCriticismBenjaminWarfield.pdf) PDF, whose text was scanned, proofread and marked up by Lance George Marshall. It has not been read against the print: **the PDF has no footnotes**, only their figures in the text, and the Greek of four passages is lost; see "Works taken from an e-text" in `docs/transcription.md`. The [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/christologycriti0000warf) is the print. Published in 1929, it is in the public domain in the United States; the committee's prefatory note and the other editorial matter of the volume may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Warfield's historico-critical articles on the person and work of Christ: the divine Messiah in the Old Testament, the people's misconception of Jesus and the charge of blasphemy, Jesus' alleged confession of sin, the article on Jesus Christ, Schmiedel's "pillar-passages", the "two natures" and recent christological speculation, "Christless Christianity" (against Drews), the twentieth-century Christ, the essence of Christianity and the cross, and an appendix on the supernatural birth of Jesus.
