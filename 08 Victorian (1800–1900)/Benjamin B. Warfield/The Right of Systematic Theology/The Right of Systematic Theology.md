# The Right of Systematic Theology

*Benjamin B. Warfield* — *The Right of Systematic Theology*, with an introduction by James Orr (Edinburgh: T. & T. Clark, 1897), from the [Monergism](https://www.monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/warfield/warfield_rightsystematic.html) text, marked up by Lance George Marshall. The text has not been read against the print: the six Greek passages were read off the page images of the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/rightofsystemat00warf), and the accented letters the e-text lost (64) were restored by inference; see "Works taken from an e-text" in `docs/transcription.md`.

The introduction by James Orr, and Warfield's article on the right of systematic theology to exist, first printed in *The Presbyterian and Reformed Review*. Against the claims that Christianity is life and not doctrine, fact and not theory, feeling and not thought, and that doctrine is only a passing form, Warfield argues from Christianity's own character: it comes as truth, makes its appeal to the mind, and so asks to be set in order. He draws on the French and German writers of the day, Sabatier, Astié, Bois, Rivier, Ritschl and others.
