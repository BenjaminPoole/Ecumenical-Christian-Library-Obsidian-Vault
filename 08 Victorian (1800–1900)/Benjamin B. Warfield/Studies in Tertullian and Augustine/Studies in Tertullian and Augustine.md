# Studies in Tertullian and Augustine

*Benjamin B. Warfield* — *Studies in Tertullian and Augustine*, volume IV of his works (New York: Oxford University Press, 1930), from the [gospelstudies.org.uk](https://gospelstudies.org.uk/earlychurch/pdf/e-books/warfield_b-b/tertullian-and-augustine_warfield.pdf) PDF. **This is an uncorrected OCR of the print**: letters are misread here and there, ligatures (*æ*, *œ*) are lost, the Greek and Hebrew are garbled, and the footnote figures in the text could not be matched to the notes, which stand at the end of each chapter in order. The title page, the committee's prefatory note and the index are left out. See "Works taken from an e-text" in `docs/transcription.md`; the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/bwb_P9-ARU-447) is the print. Published in 1930, the volume is in the public domain in the United States; the committee's editorial matter may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Five studies of the two great Latin Fathers: Tertullian and the beginnings of the doctrine of the Trinity (three articles from *The Princeton Theological Review*, 1905–1906), a sketch of Augustine (from the *Encyclopaedia of Religion and Ethics*), Augustine's doctrine of knowledge and authority, Augustine and his *Confessions*, and Augustine and the Pelagian controversy (the introductory essay of the Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers, vol. V).
