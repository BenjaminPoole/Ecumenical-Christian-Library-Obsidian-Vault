# The Lord of Glory

*Benjamin B. Warfield* — *The Lord of Glory: A Study of the Designations of Our Lord in the New Testament with Especial Reference to His Deity* (New York: American Tract Society, 1907), read again from the page images of the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/lordofglorystud00warf). The topic tags the book sets beside its paragraphs ("Pervasive Witness of N. T. to Christ") are left out, as the scan pipeline leaves out marginal tags elsewhere in the library; the footnotes are kept.

Warfield surveys every title the New Testament gives Jesus — Jesus, Christ, Lord, Son of Man, Son of God — book by book, from Mark through the Apocalypse, to show that the conviction of His deity is not a late layer laid over the gospel story but the presupposition of every part of the New Testament, the Synoptic Gospels and their supposed primitive sources included.
