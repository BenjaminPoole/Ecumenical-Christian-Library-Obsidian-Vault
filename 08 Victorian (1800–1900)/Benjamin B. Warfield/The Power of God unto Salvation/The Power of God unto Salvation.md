# The Power of God unto Salvation

*Benjamin B. Warfield* — *The Power of God unto Salvation* (Philadelphia: Presbyterian Board of Publication and Sabbath-School Work, 1903), read again from the page images of the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/powerofgodunto00warf).

Eight sermons preached in the chapel of the Theological Seminary at Princeton: on the revelation of man in Christ, the saving Christ, the argument from Christian experience, the paradox of omnipotence, the love of the Holy Ghost, the leading of the Spirit, Paul's earliest gospel in 1 Thessalonians, and false religion and the true.
