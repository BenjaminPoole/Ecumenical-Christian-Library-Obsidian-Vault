# The Saviour of the World

*Benjamin B. Warfield* — *The Saviour of the World: Sermons Preached in the Chapel of Princeton Theological Seminary* (New York: Hodder & Stoughton, George H. Doran, 1913), read again from the page images of the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/saviourofworldse00warf).

Nine sermons preached to the students of Princeton Seminary, each opening with its text: the prodigal son, the one Name under heaven, the Lamb of God, God's immeasurable love in John 3:16, the gospel of Paul, the glorified Christ, the risen Jesus, the gospel of the covenant, and the imitation of the incarnation in Philippians 2.
