# An Answer to Dr. Briggs' Shortest Catechism

*Benjamin B. Warfield* — *An Answer to Dr. Briggs' "Shortest Catechism"* (reprinted from the Detroit Free Press, 27 May 1891), transcribed from the page images of the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/answertodrbriggs0410warf).

Written on 21 May 1891 and put privately into the hands of a commissioner at the General Assembly, the paper takes the questions put to Dr. Briggs by the directors of Union Seminary, shows how each might have been framed to raise the real issues (Scripture and Reason, inspiration, the miracles, sanctification, the intermediate state) and sets beside each the answers Dr. Briggs had already printed.
