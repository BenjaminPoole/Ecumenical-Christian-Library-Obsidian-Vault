# The Westminster Assembly and Its Work

*Benjamin B. Warfield* — *The Westminster Assembly and Its Work* (Princeton: *Princeton Theological Review*, 1908), from the [Monergism](https://www.monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/warfield/warfield_westminwork.html) text, marked up by Lance George Marshall. It has not been read against the print; see "Works taken from an e-text" in `docs/transcription.md`. The [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/westmi00warf) is the print.

Two articles from *The Princeton Theological Review* of 1908. The first is the history of the calling of the Westminster Assembly: the conflict of King and Parliament over the Church, the Long Parliament, the ordinance of 1643 that convened the Divines to advise it, and the Solemn League and Covenant with the Scots that turned an advisory body into the framer of a uniform religion for three kingdoms. The second follows its work: the Directory for Church Government and for Ordination, the Directory for Worship, the Confession of Faith, the Larger and Shorter Catechisms, and what became of them.
