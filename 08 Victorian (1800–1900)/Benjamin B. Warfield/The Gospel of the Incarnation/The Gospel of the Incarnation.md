# The Gospel of the Incarnation

*Benjamin B. Warfield* — *The Gospel of the Incarnation*, two sermons preached in the Chapel of Princeton Theological Seminary on October 9, 1892, and January 8, 1893 (New York: Anson D. F. Randolph and Company, 1893), from the PDF at [theologue.wordpress.com](https://theologue.wordpress.com/) (formatted by Ed Sanders). The book is "Dedicated to the students of Princeton Seminary: for whom they were prepared, to whom they were preached, and on whose request they are now printed." It has not been read against the print: the PDF omits Warfield's footnotes and gives scripture references in modern form; see "Works taken from an e-text" in `docs/transcription.md`. The [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/gospelofincarnat00warf) is the print.

Two sermons on the incarnation. The first, on John 6:38-39, takes "the end of the incarnation": Christ came down from heaven to do the Father's will, which is the salvation of all that the Father has given Him, and the sermon draws out what this says of the covenant of redemption, the particularity of salvation, and the believer's assurance. The second, on Philippians 2:5-8, takes the example of the incarnation: the self-emptying of Christ as the pattern of the Christian's self-sacrifice.
