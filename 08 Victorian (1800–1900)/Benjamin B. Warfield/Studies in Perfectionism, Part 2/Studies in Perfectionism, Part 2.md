# Studies in Perfectionism, Part 2

*Benjamin B. Warfield* — *Studies in Perfectionism*, part 2, volume VIII of his works (New York: Oxford University Press, 1932; Monergism's front matter says 1931), from the [Monergism](https://www.monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/warfield/Vol%208_Studies%20in%20Perfectionism%20Part%202_Warfield.pdf) edition (2026; the EPUB, which has no line-end hyphens and keeps the footnote figures), whose text was scanned, proofread and marked up by Lance George Marshall. Monergism's title page is left out. Every footnote is set as a footnote. It has not been read against the print; see "Works taken from an e-text" in `docs/transcription.md`. The [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/perfectionism0002warf) is the print. Published in 1932, it is in the public domain in the United States (the user confirmed this for the volumes of 1931-32); the committee's prefatory note and the other editorial matter of the volume may still be in copyright elsewhere.

The English and American half of Warfield's study of perfectionism, the eighth volume of his works: *Oberlin Perfectionism* (Asa Mahan, Charles G. Finney and the Oberlin teaching), *John Humphrey Noyes and His "Bible Communists"* (the Oneida community), *The Mystical Perfectionism of Thomas Cogswell Upham*, *The "Higher Life" Movement* (Pearsall Smith, Boardman and the Keswick teaching) and *"The Victorious Life"*. Each first appeared in *The Princeton Theological Review*, *Bibliotheca Sacra* or *The Union Seminary Review*, and the footnotes are the study's own, with the documents.
