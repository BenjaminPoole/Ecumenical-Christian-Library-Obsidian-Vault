# Chapter 2. Theism

Theism is the doctrine of an extra-mundane, personal
God, the creator, preserver, and governor of the world. The design of all
arguments on this subject is to show that the facts around us, and the facts of
consciousness, necessitate the assumption of the existence of such a Being. The
arguments usually urged on this subject are the Ontological, the Cosmological,
the Teleological, and the Moral.
