# Chapter 6. Covenant of Works

God having created man after his own image in knowledge, righteousness, and
holiness, entered into a covenant of life with him, upon condition of perfect
obedience, forbidding him to eat of the tree of knowledge of good and evil upon
the pain of death.

According to this statement, (1.) God entered into a covenant with Adam. (2.)
The promise annexed to that covenant was life. (3.) The condition was perfect
obedience. (4.) Its penalty was death.
