# Card

The Messrs. Charles Scribner and Co., of New York, have made an
arrangement with the Messrs. Thomas Nelson and Sons, of Edinburgh, Scotland, for
the publication of this work in Great Britain. This has been done with the cordial
approbation of the author, who feels under obligation to the Messrs. Nelson for
the liberal terms on which they have undertaken the publication. The author also
desires to acknowledge his indebtedness to his accomplished young friend, Mr. Caspar
René Gregory, licentiate of the Presbytery of Philadelphia, for his assistance in
carrying the work through the press.

PRINCETON, *June* 2, 1871.
