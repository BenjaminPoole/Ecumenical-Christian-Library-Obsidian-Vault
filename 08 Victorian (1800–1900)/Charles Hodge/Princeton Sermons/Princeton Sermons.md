# Princeton Sermons

*Charles Hodge* — *Princeton Sermons: Outlines of Discourses, Doctrinal and Practical, Delivered at Princeton Theological Seminary on Sabbath Afternoons* (London: Thomas Nelson and Sons, 1879), from the Monergism ebook, checked against the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/princetonsermons00hodg).

The outlines of the addresses Hodge gave to the students of Princeton Theological Seminary on Sabbath afternoons, from 1852 to 1878, edited after his death by his son A. A. Hodge and arranged under ten heads: God and his attributes; Christ, his person and offices; the Holy Spirit; Satan, sin and sins; conversion; Christian experience and privileges; Christian duties; the means of grace; death and the consummation of redemption; and the papers of his last year. 249 outlines, each with the date it was given, together with A. A. Hodge's preface on the Sabbath conference at Princeton.
