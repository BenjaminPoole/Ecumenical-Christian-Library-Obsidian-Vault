# Essays and Reviews

*Charles Hodge* — *Essays and Reviews*, selected from the *Princeton Review* (New York: Robert Carter & Brothers, 1857), transcribed from the page images of the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/essaysreviews00hodg).

Eighteen of Hodge's reviews, on regeneration, Stuart on the Romans, the atonement, the ground of faith in the Scriptures, theories of the church, the Church of Rome, Finney's theology, the support of the clergy, Bushnell on Christian nurture, the Lord's Supper, boards of missions, slavery and emancipation, and the 'theology of the intellect and that of the feelings'.
