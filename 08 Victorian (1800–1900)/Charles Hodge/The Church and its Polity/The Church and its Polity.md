# The Church and its Polity

*Charles Hodge* — *The Church and its Polity*, edited by A. A. Hodge (London: Thomas Nelson and Sons, 1879), from the Monergism ebook, checked against the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/churchandpolity00hodguoft).

Hodge's collected writing on the doctrine and government of the Church, gathered after his death by his son A. A. Hodge from the annual articles on the General Assembly he wrote for the *Princeton Review* from 1835 and from his other articles and lectures on ecclesiology; it is the same book as *Discussions in Church Polity* (New York: Scribner, 1878). Part I sets out the principles: the idea of the Church as the communion of saints, the Oxford and Roman theories of it, its visibility and perpetuity, the principles of church union, its province, the relation of Church and State, Presbyterianism, the orders of the Church of England, and liturgies. Part II applies them to the Presbyterian constitution, from the particular church and its officers through the presbytery and the General Assembly to discipline, article by article on the questions the Assembly had to decide. The subject index at the end of the book is left out.
