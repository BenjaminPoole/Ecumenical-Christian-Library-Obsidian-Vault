# What is Darwinism

*Charles Hodge* — New York: Scribner, Armstrong and Company; London: T. Nelson and Sons, 1874, from [CCEL](https://ccel.org/ccel/hodge/darwinism).

Hodge's answer to the question in his title: not evolution as such, but natural selection as Darwin meant it — the exclusion of design from nature. "What is Darwinism? It is Atheism."
