# An Exposition of the First Epistle to the Corinthians

*Charles Hodge* — *An Exposition of the First Epistle to the Corinthians* (New York: Robert Carter & Brothers, 1857), from the [Monergism](https://www.monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/hodge/) ebook. It has not been read against the print in full; see "Works taken from an e-text" in `docs/transcription.md`. The [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/epistlecorinthians00hodgrich) is the print.

Hodge's commentary on Paul's first letter to the church at Corinth, from the author's Princeton lectures. An introduction treats Corinth, Paul's labours there, the state of the church, the date and contents of the letter and its importance; then each of the sixteen chapters is taken section by section, a summary and the verses in italics followed by the comment: the divisions and the cross as the wisdom of God, discipline and lawsuits, marriage and virgins, meats offered to idols, the Lord's Supper, spiritual gifts and the hymn to love, and the resurrection of the dead.
