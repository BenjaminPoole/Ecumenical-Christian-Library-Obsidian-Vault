# An Exposition of the Second Epistle to the Corinthians

*Charles Hodge* — *An Exposition of the Second Epistle to the Corinthians* (New York: Robert Carter & Brothers, 1859), from the [Monergism](https://www.monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/hodge/) ebook. It has not been read against the print in full; see "Works taken from an e-text" in `docs/transcription.md`. The [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/anexpositionsec00hodggoog) is the print.

Hodge's commentary on Paul's second letter to the Corinthians, the most personal of his epistles. Each of the thirteen chapters opens with a summary and is taken section by section, the verses set out and then explained: Paul's comfort in affliction and the defence of his conduct, the ministry of the new covenant and the treasure in earthen vessels, the ambassadors for Christ, the collection for the saints, and the apostle's defence of his authority against the false teachers, his visions and the thorn in the flesh.
