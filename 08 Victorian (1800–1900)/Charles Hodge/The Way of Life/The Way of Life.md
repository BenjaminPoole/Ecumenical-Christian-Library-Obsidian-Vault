# The Way of Life

*Charles Hodge* — *The Way of Life*, written for the American Sunday-School Union and revised by the Committee of Publication (Philadelphia: American Sunday-School Union, [1841]), transcribed from the page images of the [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/wayoflife00hodg).

Hodge's plain answer, written for intelligent young readers, to two questions: are the Scriptures the word of God, and what do they teach a man to believe and experience in order to be saved? Nine chapters take the reader from the evidence for Scripture through sin, indifference and conviction, to justification, faith and repentance, the profession of religion and the sacraments, and holy living.
