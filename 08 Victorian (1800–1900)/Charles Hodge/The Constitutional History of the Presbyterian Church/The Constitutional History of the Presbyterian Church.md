# The Constitutional History of the Presbyterian Church

*Charles Hodge* — *The Constitutional History of the Presbyterian Church in the United States of America* (Philadelphia: William S. Martien, 1839; Part II, 1840), from the Monergism ebook, checked against the Internet Archive scans of [part I](https://archive.org/details/constitutionalhi01hodg) and [part II](https://archive.org/details/constitutionalhih00hodg).

Hodge's history of the Presbyterian Church in America from its beginnings to 1788, written to show what its constitution was by tracing it from the origin of the churches and ministers through the adopting act of 1729, the Great Revival, the schism of 1741 and the union of 1758, in answer to the claim that American Presbyterianism rested on Congregationalism or on a looser subscription than the Westminster Confession. It first appeared in two parts, in 1839 and 1840. Each chapter opens with a summary of its argument; a list of the members of the Synod of New York and Philadelphia from 1758 to 1788 closes the book. The author's preface, the contents and the footnotes are not in the e-text, and the numerals that stand for the notes are left in the text.
