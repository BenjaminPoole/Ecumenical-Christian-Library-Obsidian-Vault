# Basel-Florence (1431)

**17th Ecumenical Council (Catholic reckoning)** · Basel · Ferrara · Florence · A.D. 1431–1445

The seventeenth ecumenical council: after the conciliarist sessions at Basel, its legitimate continuation at Ferrara and Florence proclaimed the decree of union with the Greek Church, *Laetentur caeli*, and further unions with the Armenians, Copts, and others.

Translated by Norman P. Tanner, *Decrees of the Ecumenical Councils* (Georgetown University Press, 1990). Source: [papalencyclicals.net](https://www.papalencyclicals.net/councils/ecum17.htm). This modern critical translation is under copyright and is reproduced here from papalencyclicals.net, which has hosted it freely for many years. Tanner's critical-apparatus footnotes are omitted; his introductions and the full conciliar text are retained.
