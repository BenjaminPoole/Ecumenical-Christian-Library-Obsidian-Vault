# Synod of Dort (1618)

**Reformed Synod** · Dordrecht · A.D. 1618–1619

The international synod of the Reformed churches that judged the five articles of the Arminian Remonstrance and set out the **Canons of Dort** — the doctrines of unconditional election, definite atonement, total depravity, irresistible grace, and the perseverance of the saints.

Text from Philip Schaff, *The Creeds of Christendom*, Vol. III (1877), public domain.
