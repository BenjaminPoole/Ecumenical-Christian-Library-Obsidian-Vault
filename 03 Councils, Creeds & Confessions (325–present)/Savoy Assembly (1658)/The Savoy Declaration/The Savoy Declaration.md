# The Savoy Declaration

*The Savoy Assembly of Congregational Divines (1658)* — the Savoy Declaration, drafted by John Owen, Thomas Goodwin and others, from Philip Schaff's *Creeds of Christendom* (Vol. III) via the [Christian Classics Ethereal Library](https://ccel.org/ccel/schaff/creeds3). Schaff gives the Preface and the Institution of Churches in full, and those chapters of the Confession of Faith in which Savoy differs from Westminster.
