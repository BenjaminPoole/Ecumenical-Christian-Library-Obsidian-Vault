# Diet of Regensburg (1541)

**Imperial Diet (Colloquy)** · Regensburg (Ratisbon) · A.D. 1541

The colloquy at which Catholic and Protestant theologians — Contarini, Eck, Gropper, Melanchthon, Bucer, and Pistorius — sought reconciliation. Agreement was reached on a formula of "double justification" (Article 5), but the colloquy broke down over transubstantiation, the authority of the Church, and papal jurisdiction, and neither Rome nor Luther accepted its results.
