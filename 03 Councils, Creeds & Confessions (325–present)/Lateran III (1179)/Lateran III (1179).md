# Lateran III (1179)

**11th Ecumenical Council (Catholic reckoning)** · Rome · A.D. 1179

The eleventh ecumenical council: it ended the schism of the antipopes, required a two-thirds majority of cardinals for a valid papal election, and issued twenty-seven canons.

Translated by Norman P. Tanner, *Decrees of the Ecumenical Councils* (Georgetown University Press, 1990). Source: [papalencyclicals.net](https://www.papalencyclicals.net/councils/ecum11.htm).
