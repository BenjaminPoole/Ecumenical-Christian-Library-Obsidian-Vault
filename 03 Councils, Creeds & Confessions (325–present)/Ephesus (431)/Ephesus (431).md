# Ephesus (431)

**3rd Ecumenical Council** · Ephesus · A.D. 431

The third ecumenical council: condemned Nestorius and affirmed the Theotokos.

Translated by Henry Percival, *Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers*, Second Series, Vol. 14 (1900). Source: [New Advent](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3810.htm). Public domain.
