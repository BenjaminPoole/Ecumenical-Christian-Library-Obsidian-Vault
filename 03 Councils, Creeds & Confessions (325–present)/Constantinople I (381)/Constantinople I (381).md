# Constantinople I (381)

**2nd Ecumenical Council** · Constantinople · A.D. 381

The second ecumenical council: completed the Nicene Creed and condemned the Macedonians.

Translated by Henry Percival, *Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers*, Second Series, Vol. 14 (1900). Source: [New Advent](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3808.htm). Public domain.
