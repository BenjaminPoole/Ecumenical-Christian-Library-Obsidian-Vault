# Reformation Confessions & Catechisms

Confessional standards of the Protestant Reformation, produced by the synods, assemblies, and convocations of the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries. Each is given here in an authoritative public-domain English text.

## Anglican (English Reformation)

- [[Thirty-Nine Articles of Religion|The Thirty-Nine Articles of Religion]] (1571) — the doctrinal articles of the Church of England, in their original English form. *39 articles.*

## Continental Reformed

- [[Heidelberg Catechism|The Heidelberg Catechism]] (1563) — the gentlest and most beloved of the Reformed catechisms, one of the Three Forms of Unity. *129 questions in 52 Lord's Days.*

## The Westminster Standards

Produced by the Westminster Assembly (1643–53) and adopted across the Presbyterian and Reformed churches.

- [[The Westminster Confession of Faith|The Westminster Confession of Faith]] (1646) — the confession proper, in *33 chapters*. Held with the Assembly's other work under [[Westminster Assembly|Puritans → Westminster Assembly]], in Schaff's text (*Creeds of Christendom* III, via CCEL).
- [[The Westminster Shorter Catechism|The Westminster Shorter Catechism]] (1647) — *107 questions*, for the instruction of the young and unlearned. Also held under [[Westminster Assembly|Puritans → Westminster Assembly]].
- [[Westminster Larger Catechism|The Westminster Larger Catechism]] (1648) — *196 questions*, a fuller exposition for public teaching.
