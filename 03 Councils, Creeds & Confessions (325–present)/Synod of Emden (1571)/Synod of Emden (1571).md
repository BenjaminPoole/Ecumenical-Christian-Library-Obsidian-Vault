# Synod of Emden (1571)

**Reformed Synod** · Emden · A.D. 1571

The first general synod of the Dutch Reformed churches, meeting in exile at Emden in East Frisia. Its acts established the presbyterian-synodal church order — consistories, classes, and synods — that shaped the government of the Reformed Church of the Netherlands.
