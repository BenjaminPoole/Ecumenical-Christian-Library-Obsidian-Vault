# Vienne (1311)

**15th Ecumenical Council (Catholic reckoning)** · Vienne · A.D. 1311–1312

The fifteenth ecumenical council: it suppressed the Order of the Temple, condemned the errors of the Beguines and Beghards, and issued the decrees later gathered as the *Clementine Constitutions*.

Translated by Norman P. Tanner, *Decrees of the Ecumenical Councils* (Georgetown University Press, 1990). Source: [papalencyclicals.net](https://www.papalencyclicals.net/councils/ecum15.htm). Tanner's critical-apparatus footnotes are omitted; his introductions and the full conciliar text are retained.
