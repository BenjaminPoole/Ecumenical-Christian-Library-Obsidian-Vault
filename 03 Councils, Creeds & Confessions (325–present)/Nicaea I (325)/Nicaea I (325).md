# Nicaea I (325)

**1st Ecumenical Council** · Nicaea (Iznik) · A.D. 325

The first ecumenical council: condemned Arianism, promulgated the Nicene Creed and twenty canons.

Translated by Henry Percival, *Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers*, Second Series, Vol. 14 (1900). Source: [New Advent](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3801.htm). Public domain.
