# Vatican I (1869)

**20th Ecumenical Council (Catholic reckoning)** · Rome · A.D. 1869–1870

The twentieth ecumenical council: it defined the dogmatic constitution *Dei Filius* on the Catholic faith and reason, and *Pastor Aeternus* on the primacy and infallibility of the Roman pontiff.

Translated by Norman P. Tanner, *Decrees of the Ecumenical Councils* (Georgetown University Press, 1990). Source: [papalencyclicals.net](https://www.papalencyclicals.net/councils/ecum20.htm). This modern critical translation is under copyright and is reproduced here from papalencyclicals.net, which has hosted it freely for many years. Tanner's critical-apparatus footnotes are omitted; his introductions and the full conciliar text are retained.

## Documents

1. [[Vatican I — Sessions 1 and 2|Sessions 1 and 2]] — The decree of opening (session 1) and the profession of faith (session 2).
2. [[Vatican I — Dei Filius|Dei Filius]] — *Dei Filius* — the Dogmatic Constitution on the Catholic Faith (session 3), in four chapters with canons, on God, revelation, faith, and faith and reason.
3. [[Vatican I — Pastor Aeternus|Pastor Aeternus]] — *Pastor Aeternus* — the First Dogmatic Constitution on the Church of Christ (session 4), in four chapters, defining the primacy and the infallible teaching office of the pope.
