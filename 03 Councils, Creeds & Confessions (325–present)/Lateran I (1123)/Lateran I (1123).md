# Lateran I (1123)

**9th Ecumenical Council (Catholic reckoning)** · Rome · A.D. 1123

The ninth ecumenical council and the first held in the West: it confirmed the Concordat of Worms on lay investiture and enacted canons of ecclesiastical reform.

Translated by Norman P. Tanner, *Decrees of the Ecumenical Councils* (Georgetown University Press, 1990). Source: [papalencyclicals.net](https://www.papalencyclicals.net/councils/ecum09.htm).
