# Lateran IV (1215)

**12th Ecumenical Council (Catholic reckoning)** · Rome · A.D. 1215

The twelfth ecumenical council, the greatest of the medieval councils: it defined transubstantiation, required annual confession and communion, and issued seventy reforming constitutions and the crusade decree *Ad liberandam*.

Translated by Norman P. Tanner, *Decrees of the Ecumenical Councils* (Georgetown University Press, 1990). Source: [papalencyclicals.net](https://www.papalencyclicals.net/councils/ecum12-2.htm). Tanner's critical-apparatus footnotes are omitted; his introductions and the full conciliar text are retained.
