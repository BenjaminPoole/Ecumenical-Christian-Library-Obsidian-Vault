# Westminster Assembly

The Westminster Assembly of Divines (1643–1649) produced the confession and catechisms that became the doctrinal standards of Presbyterian and Reformed churches throughout the English-speaking world.

## Works (2)

- **[[The Westminster Confession of Faith|The Westminster Confession of Faith]]** — The confession of the Westminster Assembly (1646), the classic Reformed standard. (33 notes)
- **[[The Westminster Shorter Catechism|The Westminster Shorter Catechism]]** — The Assembly's catechism of the faith in question and answer. (1 notes)
