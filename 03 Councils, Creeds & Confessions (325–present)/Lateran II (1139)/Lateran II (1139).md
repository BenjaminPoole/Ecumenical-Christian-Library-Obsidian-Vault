# Lateran II (1139)

**10th Ecumenical Council (Catholic reckoning)** · Rome · A.D. 1139

The tenth ecumenical council: it ended the papal schism of Anacletus II and promulgated thirty canons of reform, including the invalidity of the marriages of those in major orders.

Translated by Norman P. Tanner, *Decrees of the Ecumenical Councils* (Georgetown University Press, 1990). Source: [papalencyclicals.net](https://www.papalencyclicals.net/councils/ecum10.htm).
