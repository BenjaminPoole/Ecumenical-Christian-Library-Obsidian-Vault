# Diet of Augsburg (1530)

**Imperial Diet** · Augsburg · A.D. 1530

The imperial diet at which the Lutheran estates presented the **Augsburg Confession** (25 June 1530), the primary confession of the Lutheran Church, drawn up by Philipp Melanchthon. The Roman theologians answered with the *Confutatio Pontificia*, and Melanchthon in turn defended the Confession in the **Apology**.

Both texts are already in the vault, under Melanchthon (Bente–Dau translation).
