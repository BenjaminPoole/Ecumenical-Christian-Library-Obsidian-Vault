# Constance (1414)

**16th Ecumenical Council (Catholic reckoning)** · Constance · A.D. 1414–1418

The sixteenth ecumenical council: it ended the Great Western Schism, condemned John Wyclif and Jan Hus, and passed the decrees *Haec sancta* and *Frequens*.

Translated by Norman P. Tanner, *Decrees of the Ecumenical Councils* (Georgetown University Press, 1990). Source: [papalencyclicals.net](https://www.papalencyclicals.net/councils/ecum16.htm). This modern critical translation is under copyright and is reproduced here from papalencyclicals.net, which has hosted it freely for many years. Tanner's critical-apparatus footnotes are omitted; his introductions and the full conciliar text are retained.

## Documents

1. [[Constance — Sessions 1 to 4|Sessions 1 to 4]] — Two introductions and sessions 1–4, the opening of the council and the flight of John XXIII.
2. [[Constance — Haec Sancta (Session 5)|Haec Sancta (Session 5)]] — The decree *Haec sancta* asserting the authority of a general council over the pope (session 5, 6 April 1415), with the brief sessions 6–7.
3. [[Constance — Condemnation of Wyclif (Session 8)|Condemnation of Wyclif (Session 8)]] — The condemnation of the 260 articles of John Wyclif and his books (session 8, 4 May 1415).
4. [[Constance — Sessions 9 to 14|Sessions 9 to 14]] — Sessions 9–14, including the deposition of Pope John XXIII and the council's acceptance of Gregory XII's resignation.
5. [[Constance — Sentence Against Jan Hus (Session 15)|Sentence Against Jan Hus (Session 15)]] — The sentence and degradation of Jan Hus, condemned to be burned at the stake (session 15, 6 July 1415), together with further articles of Wyclif condemned in the same session.
6. [[Constance — Sessions 16 to 37|Sessions 16 to 37]] — Sessions 16–37 (July 1415 – July 1417), the procedural sessions of the council's middle years, including the treaty of Narbonne and the withdrawal of Aragon's obedience from the Avignon claimant Benedict XIII.
7. [[Constance — Frequens (Session 39)|Frequens (Session 39)]] — The decree *Frequens*, requiring general councils to be held at regular intervals (session 39, 9 October 1417), preceded by the brief session 38.
8. [[Constance — Sessions 40 to 45|Sessions 40 to 45]] — Sessions 40–45, including the election of Pope Martin V (session 41) and the dissolution of the council.
