# Lyons I (1245)

**13th Ecumenical Council (Catholic reckoning)** · Lyons · A.D. 1245

The thirteenth ecumenical council: it deposed the excommunicated emperor Frederick II and enacted constitutions on ecclesiastical procedure and aid for the Holy Land and the Latin empire.

Translated by Norman P. Tanner, *Decrees of the Ecumenical Councils* (Georgetown University Press, 1990). Source: [papalencyclicals.net](https://www.papalencyclicals.net/councils/ecum13.htm). Tanner's critical-apparatus footnotes are omitted; his introductions and the full conciliar text are retained.
