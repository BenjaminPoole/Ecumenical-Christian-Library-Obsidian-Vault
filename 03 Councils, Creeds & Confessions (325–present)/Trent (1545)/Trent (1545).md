# Trent (1545)

**19th Ecumenical Council (Catholic reckoning)** · Trent · A.D. 1545–1563

The nineteenth ecumenical council and the definitive council of the Catholic Counter-Reformation: over twenty-five sessions under three popes it defined the Catholic doctrines of Scripture and tradition, original sin, justification, and the seven sacraments in answer to the Reformation, and enacted a sweeping programme of church reform.

Translated by J. Waterworth, *The Canons and Decrees of the Sacred and Oecumenical Council of Trent* (London: C. Dolman, 1848). Source: [Hanover Historical Texts Project](https://history.hanover.edu/texts/trent.html). Public domain. Waterworth's bracketed page numbers have been removed and his prefatory essay is not included; the conciliar text is given in full.
