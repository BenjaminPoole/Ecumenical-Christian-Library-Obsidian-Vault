# Convocation of 1563

**Convocation of Canterbury** · London · A.D. 1563

The convocation of the province of Canterbury that revised and adopted the Articles of Religion of the Church of England, reducing them to the form later completed as the **Thirty-Nine Articles** (finalised 1571).

The Articles themselves are in the vault, under Reformation Confessions.
