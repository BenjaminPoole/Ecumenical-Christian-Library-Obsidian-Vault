# Constantinople III (680)

**6th Ecumenical Council** · Constantinople · A.D. 680–681

The sixth ecumenical council: condemned Monothelitism, affirming two wills in Christ.

Translated by Henry Percival, *Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers*, Second Series, Vol. 14 (1900). Source: [New Advent](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3813.htm). Public domain.
