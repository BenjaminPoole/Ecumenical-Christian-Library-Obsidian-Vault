# Lateran V (1512)

**18th Ecumenical Council (Catholic reckoning)** · Rome · A.D. 1512–1517

The eighteenth ecumenical council: it annulled the schismatic council of Pisa, defined the immortality of the individual human soul, and decreed measures of church reform on the eve of the Reformation.

Translated by Norman P. Tanner, *Decrees of the Ecumenical Councils* (Georgetown University Press, 1990). Source: [papalencyclicals.net](https://www.papalencyclicals.net/councils/ecum18.htm). Tanner's critical-apparatus footnotes are omitted; his introductions and the full conciliar text are retained.
