# Lyons II (1274)

**14th Ecumenical Council (Catholic reckoning)** · Lyons · A.D. 1274

The fourteenth ecumenical council: it achieved a short-lived reunion with the Greek Church, regulated papal elections by the constitution on conclaves, and issued constitutions of reform.

Translated by Norman P. Tanner, *Decrees of the Ecumenical Councils* (Georgetown University Press, 1990). Source: [papalencyclicals.net](https://www.papalencyclicals.net/councils/ecum14.htm).
