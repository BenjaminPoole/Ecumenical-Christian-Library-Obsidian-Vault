# Constantinople IV (869)

**8th Ecumenical Council (Catholic reckoning)** · Constantinople · A.D. 869–870

The eighth ecumenical council in the Catholic reckoning: it condemned and deposed the patriarch Photius, confirming the restoration of Ignatius, and issued twenty-seven canons.

Translated by Norman P. Tanner, *Decrees of the Ecumenical Councils* (Georgetown University Press, 1990). Source: [papalencyclicals.net](https://www.papalencyclicals.net/councils/ecum08.htm).
