# Certain Difficulties Felt by Anglicans in Catholic Teaching

Volume 1 is the twelve lectures of 1850 addressed to the followers of the Oxford Movement; Volume 2 holds the *Letter to Pusey* on his *Eirenicon* (1865) and the *Letter to the Duke of Norfolk* (1875), Newman's reply to Gladstone on papal infallibility and conscience.
