# The Via Media of the Anglican Church

The two volumes in which Newman, as a Catholic, republished his Anglican case for a middle way between Rome and popular Protestantism: the *Lectures on the Prophetical Office of the Church* (1837) with the 1877 Preface answering them, and the occasional letters and tracts, Tract 90 among them.
