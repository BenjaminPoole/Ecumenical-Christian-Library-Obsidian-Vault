# Dedication

TO MY DEAR AND MUCH-ADMIRED

ISAAC WILLIAMS, B.D.

FELLOW OF TRINITY COLLEGE, OXFORD:

THE SIGHT OF WHOM

CARRIES BACK HIS FRIENDS

TO ANCIENT, HOLY, AND HAPPY TIMES.

*Feb*. 21, 1840.
