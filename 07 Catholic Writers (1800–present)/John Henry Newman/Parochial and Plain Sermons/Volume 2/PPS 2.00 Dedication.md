# Dedication

TO

JOHN WILLIAM BOWDEN, ESQ.

ETC., ETC.

IN THE CHEERFUL CONVICTION

THAT THE ENGLISH CHURCH AMID MANY DEFECTIONS

STILL HOLDS HER INFLUENCE

OVER AN ATTACHED AND ZEALOUS LAITY,

THIS VOLUME

IS INSCRIBED,

BY HIS AFFECTIONATE FRIEND,

J. H. N.

Feb. 21st, 1835
