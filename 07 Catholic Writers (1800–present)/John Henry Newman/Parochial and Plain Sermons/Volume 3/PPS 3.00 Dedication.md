# Dedication

TO THE VENERABLE

**ROBERT HURRELL FROUDE,**

ARCHDEACON OF TOTNES,

THE FOLLOWING VOLUME,

WITH EVERY FEELING

OF ESTEEM AND ATTACHMENT,

AND WITH A GRATEFUL REMEMBRANCE

OF MANY KINDNESSES RECEIVED,

IS RESPECTFULLY INSCRIBED,

BY

THE AUTHOR.

*The Conversion of St. Paul*,  
1836\.
