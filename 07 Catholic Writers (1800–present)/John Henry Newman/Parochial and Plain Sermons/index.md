# Parochial and Plain Sermons

Newman's Anglican parish sermons, preached at St Mary the Virgin, Oxford, and at Littlemore, and published as six volumes of *Parochial Sermons* (1834–1842) and a volume of *Plain Sermons* (1843); collected in eight volumes by W. J. Copeland in 1868.
