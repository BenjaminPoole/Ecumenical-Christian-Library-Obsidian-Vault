# The Marrow of Sacred Divinity

*William Ames* — the anonymous 1642 English translation of Ames' *Medulla Theologiae* (1627); from the [Text Creation Partnership](https://textcreationpartnership.org)'s transcription of that edition, keyed by hand from the page images.

Ames opens with the definition that governs everything after it — *divinity is the doctrine of living to God* — and builds the whole of theology on it in two books: the first on faith in God, from his essence and decree through creation, the fall, Christ and the application of redemption to the church and sacraments; the second on observance, which is that faith lived. Written in Franeker for students, it was the textbook of New England divinity for a century.

This is the seventeenth-century English *Marrow of Sacred Divinity*, not the modern translation published as *The Marrow of Theology*, which is in copyright.
