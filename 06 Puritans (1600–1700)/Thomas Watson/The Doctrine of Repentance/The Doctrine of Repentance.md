# The Doctrine of Repentance

*Thomas Watson* — from the [Text Creation Partnership](https://textcreationpartnership.org)'s transcription of the 1668 edition, keyed by hand from the page images.

Watson preaches repentance from Acts 26:20, taking it apart into six ingredients — sight of sin, sorrow for sin, confession, shame, hatred, and turning — and then pressing it home with motives, cautions and means.
