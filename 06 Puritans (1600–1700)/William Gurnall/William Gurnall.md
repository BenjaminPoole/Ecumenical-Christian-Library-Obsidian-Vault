# William Gurnall

William Gurnall (1616–1679), rector of Lavenham in Suffolk, is remembered for one immense and richly practical work on spiritual warfare.

## Works (1)

- **[[The Christian in Complete Armour|The Christian in Complete Armour]]** — A vast exposition of Ephesians 6:10-20 on the Christian's spiritual warfare. (18 notes)
