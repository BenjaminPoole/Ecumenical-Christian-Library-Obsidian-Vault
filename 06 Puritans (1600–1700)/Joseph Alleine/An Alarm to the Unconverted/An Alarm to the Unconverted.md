# An Alarm to the Unconverted

*Joseph Alleine* — read again from the page images of the 1850 American Tract Society edition ([archive.org](https://archive.org/details/alarmtounconvert00alleiala)), in modern spelling.

Alleine wrote this in 1671, three years after being turned out of Taunton and imprisoned for preaching, and died at thirty-four. It went through edition after edition — twenty thousand copies of the first, fifty thousand of the second — and it is the book that stands behind Baxter's *Call to the Unconverted* on one side and Whitefield's preaching on the other. It asks one question and will not let it go: what conversion is not, what it is, why it is necessary, the marks of the unconverted, their misery, the directions, and the motives.
