# William Perkins

William Perkins (1558–1602), fellow of Christ's College and lecturer at Great St Andrew's in Cambridge, is the father of the English Puritan tradition — the elder to whom nearly everyone else in this section owes a method. He died before most of them were born, and his works outsold Calvin's in England for a generation.

## Works (1)

- **[[The Art of Prophesying|The Art of Prophesying]]** — The first Protestant manual of preaching in English: read the text, give the sense, gather the doctrine, apply it to the seven kinds of hearer. (14 notes)
