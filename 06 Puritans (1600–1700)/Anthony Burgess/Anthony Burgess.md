# Anthony Burgess

Anthony Burgess (d. 1664), fellow of Emmanuel College and vicar of Sutton Coldfield, sat in the Westminster Assembly and preached to it. He wrote at length and against everybody — Antinomians, Arminians, Socinians, Papists — but always to a pastoral end, and his hundred and twenty sermons on assurance are the fullest thing the Puritans left on how a man may know he is in a state of grace. He was ejected in 1662.
