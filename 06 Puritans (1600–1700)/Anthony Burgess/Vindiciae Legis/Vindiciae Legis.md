# Vindiciae Legis

*Anthony Burgess* — from the [Text Creation Partnership](https://textcreationpartnership.org)'s transcription of the 1647 second edition, keyed by hand from the page images.

*Vindiciae Legis, or A Vindication of the Morall Law and the Covenants* is twenty-nine lectures given at Lawrence-Jury in 1646, against the Antinomians of the day — and behind them the Papists, Arminians and Socinians. Burgess takes the law through every state it has stood in: the law of nature written on the heart, the law given to Adam and the covenant of works, the moral law promulgated by Moses, Christ's exposition of it in the Sermon on the Mount, and the difference between the law and the gospel. It is the fullest Westminster-era defence of the third use of the law.
