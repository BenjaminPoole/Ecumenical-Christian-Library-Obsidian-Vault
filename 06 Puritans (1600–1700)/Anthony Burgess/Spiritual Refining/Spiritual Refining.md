# Spiritual Refining

*Anthony Burgess* — from the [Text Creation Partnership](https://textcreationpartnership.org)'s transcription of the 1652 edition, keyed by hand from the page images.

*Spiritual Refining, or A Treatise of Grace and Assurance* is a hundred and twenty sermons, and the fullest treatment of assurance the Puritans produced. Burgess sets out the true signs of grace, spends fifteen sermons on the false ones, and then works through the nature of grace itself under each of the names Scripture gives it — the new birth, the new creature, God's workmanship, sanctification, conversion, the heart of flesh, the indwelling Spirit, and effectual calling. Burgess sat in the Westminster Assembly; this is the divinity of the Confession preached.
