# Richard Baxter

Richard Baxter (1615–1691), pastor of Kidderminster, was one of the most influential and prolific of the Puritan pastors.

## Works (3)

- **[[A Call to the Unconverted|A Call to the Unconverted]]** — An earnest evangelistic appeal to turn and live. (6 notes)
- **[[The Reformed Pastor|The Reformed Pastor]]** — His searching call to faithful, personal pastoral ministry. (19 notes)
- **[[The Saints' Everlasting Rest|The Saints' Everlasting Rest]]** — A classic meditation on the believer's heavenly rest. (19 notes)
