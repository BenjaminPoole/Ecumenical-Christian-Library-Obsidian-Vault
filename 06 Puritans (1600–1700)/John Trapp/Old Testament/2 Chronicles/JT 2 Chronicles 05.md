# 2 Chronicles 5

**Ver. 1.** Thus all the work.] See 1 Kings 7:51.

**Ver. 2.** Then Solomon assembled, &c.] See for this whole chapter following, 1 Kings 8:1-12. *[See Trapp on "1Ki 8:1"]* &c

**Ver. 3.** In the seventh month.] Called Ethanim: haply from the many festivals therein observed. And Solomon fitly taketh that time for the dedication of the temple.

**Ver. 9.** And there it is unto this day,] i.e., Until the time that the larger Chronicles of the Kings of Judah out of which Ezra compiled this abstract—were written.

**Ver. 11.** And did not then wait by course.] Because the work was extraordinary.

**Ver. 12.** Being arrayed in white linen.] Which might remind them of that innoceney and integrity required of them.

**Ver. 13.** Were even as one, to make one sound.] Signifying that unanimity and good agreement that must be amongst ministers, yea, all Christians.

**Ver. 14.** So that the priests could not stand.] This showed that that Levitical ministry should cease when the Lord Christ came.
