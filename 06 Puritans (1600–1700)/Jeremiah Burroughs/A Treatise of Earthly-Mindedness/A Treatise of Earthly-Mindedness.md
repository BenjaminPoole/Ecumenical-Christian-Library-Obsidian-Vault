# A Treatise of Earthly-Mindedness

*Jeremiah Burroughs* — from the [Text Creation Partnership](https://textcreationpartnership.org)'s transcription of the 1652 *Two Treatises of Mr. Jeremiah Burroughs*, keyed by hand from the page images.

Preached on Philippians 3:19, *who mind earthly things*. Burroughs discovers earthly-mindedness in nine particulars, presses fourteen evils of it, gives seven reasons why men are earthly-minded, and closes with considerations and directions for getting the heart free. Its companion, [[An Heavenly Conversation|An Heavenly Conversation]], was printed with it. Spelling and punctuation are as printed in 1652.
