# Of Walking with God

*Jeremiah Burroughs* — from the [Text Creation Partnership](https://textcreationpartnership.org)'s transcription of the 1652 *Two Treatises of Mr. Jeremiah Burroughs*, keyed by hand from the page images.

A third treatise bound with the two on earthly-mindedness and heavenly conversation, preached on Genesis 5:24, *and Enoch walked with God*. Burroughs opens what walking with God is in nine particulars, sets out twelve excellencies and ten evidences of it, gives twelve rules of direction, and answers the objection of God hiding his face.
