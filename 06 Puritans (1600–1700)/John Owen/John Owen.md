# John Owen

John Owen (1616–1683), the greatest of the English Puritan theologians, was Vice-Chancellor of Oxford and the leading Independent divine of his age. His complete works are gathered here.

## Works (32)

- **[[Brief Instruction in the Worship of God|Brief Instruction in the Worship of God]]** — A catechism on gospel worship and the ordinances of the church. (54 notes)
- **[[Death of Death in the Death of Christ|Death of Death in the Death of Christ]]** — His classic defense of particular redemption — Christ's death actually saves those for whom he died. (40 notes)

- **[[Dissertation on Divine Justice|Dissertation on Divine Justice]]** — On the necessity of divine vindicatory justice. (22 notes)
