# Question 49 — What is the duty of the elders of the church towards persons desiring to be admitted unto the fellowship of the church?

Answer — [^a1]To discern and judge by the rule of truth, applied in love, between sincere professors and hypocritical pretenders; [^b1]to influence, direct, comfort, and encourage in the way, such as they judge to love the Lord Jesus in sincerity; [^c1]to propose and recommend them unto the whole church, with prayers and supplications to God for them; [^d1]to admit them, being approved, into the order and fellowship of the gospel in the church.

[^a1]: Acts viii. 20, 23; Titus i. 10; Rev. ii. 2; Jer. xv. 19.

[^b1]: Acts xviii. 26; 1 Thess. ii. 7, 8, 11.

[^c1]: Acts ix. 27, 28.

[^d1]: Rom. xiv. 1.
