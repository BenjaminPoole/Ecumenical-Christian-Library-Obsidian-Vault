# Question 38 — Who are the principal subjects of baptism?

Question 38 — Who are the principal subjects of baptism?

Answer — Professing believers, if not baptized in their infancy, and their infant seed. Matt. xxviii. 19; Acts ii. 38, 39, xvi. 33; 1 Cor. i. 16, vii. 14; Col. ii. 12–14, with Gen. xvii. 10–12.
