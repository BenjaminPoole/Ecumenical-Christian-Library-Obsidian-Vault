# Question 44 — May the church cast any person out of its communion without previous admonition?

Question 44 — May the church cast any person out of its communion without previous admonition?

Answer — It may in some cases, where the offence is notorious and the scandal grievous, so that nothing be done against other general rules. 1 Cor. v.
