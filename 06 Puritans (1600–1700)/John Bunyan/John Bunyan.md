# John Bunyan

John Bunyan (1628–1688), the Bedford tinker and preacher, wrote from prison the most beloved allegories in the English language.

## Works (3)

- **[[Grace Abounding to the Chief of Sinners|Grace Abounding to the Chief of Sinners]]** — His spiritual autobiography of conviction and conversion. (6 notes)
- **[[The Holy War|The Holy War]]** — The allegory of the town of Mansoul, besieged and reclaimed by Emmanuel. (21 notes)
- **[[The Pilgrim's Progress|The Pilgrim's Progress]]** — The immortal allegory of the Christian's journey to the Celestial City. (21 notes)
