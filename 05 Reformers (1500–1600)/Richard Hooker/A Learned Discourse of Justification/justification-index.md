# A Learned Discourse of Justification, Works, and How the Foundation of Faith is Overthrown

Hooker's sermon on justification, preached at the Temple Church in 1585 and published posthumously. Its argument that faithful members of the medieval church might be saved provoked the controversy with Walter Travers.
