# Of the Laws of Ecclesiastical Polity

Hooker's eight books on church polity - the classic defence of the Elizabethan settlement against its puritan critics, and the foundation of Anglican theological method. Books I-IV appeared in 1593, Book V in 1597; Books VI and VIII were published posthumously in 1648, Book VII in 1662, from papers of disputed completeness. Text from John Keble's seventh edition (Clarendon Press, 1888), via the Online Library of Liberty; Keble's scholarly footnotes are retained.
