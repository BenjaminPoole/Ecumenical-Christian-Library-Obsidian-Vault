And yet the controversy here in this matter is, not what may be, but what is.

“God can do many things, which he neither doth nor will do.” And to us his will, in things that appear not to our senses, is not known but by his word. Christ's body may be as well in the bread and wine, as in the door and stone; and yet it may be also in the door and stone, and not in the bread and wine.
