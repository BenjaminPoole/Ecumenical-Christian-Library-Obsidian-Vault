# Writings and Disputations relative to the Lord's Supper

Parker Society vol. 1 (1844). The bulk of the volume is Cranmer's *Answer unto a Crafty and Sophistical Cavillation devised by Stephen Gardiner* (1551), reprinted from the final, most-corrected 1580 edition. As printed there it runs in five books, but not in numerical order - I, III, IV, II, V - each book opening with Cranmer's confutation of the corresponding part of Gardiner's *Explication*, which attacked the books of Cranmer's original 1550 *Defence* in that sequence. Books III (Of the Presence of Christ) and II (Against Transubstantiation) are much the longest and are split into sized parts; the rest run as single notes. After the five books come the Answer to Richard Smith's preface, a note of where Gardiner varied from other papists, the 1554 Oxford disputations, and the volume's index. The volume closes with a genuine second work: the Latin *Defensio Verae et Catholicae Doctrinae de Sacramento* (Emden, 1557) - Cranmer's own first, Latin, statement of the doctrine, in its own five books (Libri I-V). Printed section headings are kept inline throughout.
