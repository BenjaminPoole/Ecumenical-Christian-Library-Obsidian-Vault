# 8. Some Queries Concerning Confirmation, with Cranmer's Answers

First, for the places alleged for the same be no institutions, but acts Hist, Of the apostles. var — Second, these acts were done by a special gift given to the apostles for Ed firmation of God's word at that time.

Thirdly, the said special gift doth not now remain with the successors apostles.

What is the external sign?

The church useth chrisma for the exterior sign, but the scripture maketh tion thereof.:

What is the efficacy of this sacrament?

The bishop, in the name of the church, doth invocate the Holy Ghost strength and constancy, with other spiritual gifts, unto the person confirmed the efficacy of this sacrament is of such value as is the prayer of the bishop in the name of the church.
