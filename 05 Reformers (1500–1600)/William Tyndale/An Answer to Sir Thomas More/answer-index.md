# An Answer to Sir Thomas More's Dialogue

Parker Society vol. 3 (1850), ed. Henry Walter. Tyndale's reply opens with an introductory notice and preface, then a set of foundational treatises (what the church is, the senses of scripture, and the like) before answering More's *Dialogue* book by book - First through Fourth. Printed with it: *The Supper of the Lord* (with its own introductory notice), Tyndale's exposition of William Tracy's testament (with the testament itself and Tyndale's exposition of it as separate pieces), specimens of Tyndale's Bible translation, and the volume's two indexes (scripture texts and general).
