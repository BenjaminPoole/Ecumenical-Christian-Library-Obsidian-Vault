IN that St John saith, “The thing which was from the beginning," and “the everlasting life that was with the Father," he witnesseth that Christ is very God; as he doth in the beginning of his gospel, saying: The word," or the thing?, was at the beginning, and the thing was with Godand that thing was God, and all things were made by it."

And when he saith, Which we heard, and saw with our eyes, and our hands handled" him; he testifieth that Christ is very man also; as he doth in the beginning of his gospel saying: “The word," or that thing, “was made flesh," that is, became man. And thus we have in plain and open words a manifest article of our faith, that our Saviour Christ is very God and very man.
