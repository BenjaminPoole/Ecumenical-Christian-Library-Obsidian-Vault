# Expositions and Notes on Sundry Portions of the Holy Scriptures

Parker Society vol. 2 (1849), ed. Henry Walter: Tyndale's exposition of Matthew v-vii - the Sermon on the Mount - with its prologue, split by chapter; his exposition of the First Epistle of John, with its introductory notice, prologue, and text split by chapter; his marginal notes on Matthew i-xxi; and *The Practice of Prelates*, with its own introductory notice and preface. (This volume does not in fact contain *A Pathway into the Holy Scripture* - that appears only in passing reference here; it is not among the pieces actually printed in this volume.)
