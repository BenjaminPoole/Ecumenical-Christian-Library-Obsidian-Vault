# First Principles of the Reformation

*First Principles of the Reformation, or the Ninety-five Theses and the Three Primary Works of Dr. Martin Luther* (ed. Henry Wace and C. A. Buchheim, London, 1883). Gathers the Ninety-five Theses (1517) with the three great treatises of 1520 — *To the Christian Nobility of the German Nation*, *Concerning Christian Liberty*, and *On the Babylonish Captivity of the Church* — together with the editors' introductory essays.
