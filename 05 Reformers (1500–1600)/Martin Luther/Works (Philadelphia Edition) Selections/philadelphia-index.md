# Works of Martin Luther (Philadelphia Edition) - further treatises

Treatises from volumes I-II of the Philadelphia (Holman) edition not otherwise in this collection: the prefaces, Baptism, Confession, the Fourteen of Consolation, the New Testament (Holy Mass), the Papacy at Rome, the Blessed Sacrament, the Ban, the Brief Explanation, and the Eight Wittenberg Sermons. Works already present elsewhere (Ninety-five Theses, Good Works, Christian Nobility, Babylonian Captivity, Christian Liberty) are not repeated. Each work opens with its editor's historical introduction.
