## Overlap with other editions

> [!info] Same text elsewhere in this library
> Sections **14–25** are the Lenker translation of Luther's sermon on 2 Corinthians 3:4–11, which also appears whole as [[Luther ES3 13 Twelfth Sunday After Trinity - 2 Corinthians 3, 4-11|Twelfth Sunday After Trinity]] in [[epistle-sermons-index|Epistle Sermons: Trinity to Advent]].
> Sections **28–35** are likewise his sermon on Philippians 3:17–21, held whole as [[Luther ES3 24 Twenty Third Sunday After Trinity - Philippians 3, 17-21|Twenty-Third Sunday After Trinity]].
> Both editions are kept: this one is CCEL's *Assorted Sermons* (split by section), the other Gutenberg's *Epistle Sermons* (whole sermons).
