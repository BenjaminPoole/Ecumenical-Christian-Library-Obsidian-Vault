# Protestant Reformers

Works of the Protestant Reformers, from Wycliffe and Hus in the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries through the magisterial reformers of the sixteenth. Each text names its translator and edition in the note frontmatter; where a work exists only in an older translation, that is stated rather than glossed over.

## Known gaps

What exists here is bounded by what has been translated into English and is freely reproducible. The main omissions: the bulk of the Weimar Luther (only ~15 major works have public-domain English); Melanchthon's *Loci Communes* (all English translations remain in copyright); most of Beza's corpus (never translated); Wycliffe's Latin works (untranslated) and the full Middle English sermon corpus; Knox's Works vols. 3-6 (occasional writings). Bullinger's Second Helvetic Confession is under Church Councils → Reformation Confessions.
