# Philipp Melanchthon (1497–1560)

Luther's closest colleague at Wittenberg and the systematiser of Lutheran theology. Author of the Augsburg Confession and its Apology, and of the *Loci Communes*, the first Protestant systematic theology. Called *Praeceptor Germaniae*, the teacher of Germany.
