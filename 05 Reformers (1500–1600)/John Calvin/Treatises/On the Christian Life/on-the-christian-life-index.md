# On the Christian Life

Calvin's classic short treatise on Christian living (*De vita hominis christiani*), long circulated on its own as the “Golden Booklet of the True Christian Life.” The text is Institutes Book III, chapters 6–10, in Henry Beveridge's 1845 translation, here in CCEL's standalone edition (chr_life.xml). See also the same material in its home context, beginning at [[Inst III.6 The Life of a Christian Man|Institutes III.6]].
