# Catechism of the Church of Geneva

Calvin's 1545 catechism, “a form of instruction for children in the doctrine of Christ,” cast as a dialogue between Master and Scholar in five parts. Translated by Henry Beveridge for the Calvin Translation Society (*Tracts Relating to the Reformation*, vol. 2, 1849). Converted from uncorrected archive.org OCR of the printed volume: mechanical noise (running heads, line breaks) has been removed by script, but occasional OCR character errors may remain in the text.
