# Treatises

Calvin's shorter theological and polemical works, in Henry Beveridge's
translations. The CCEL-sourced texts are clean transcriptions; the works
taken from the Calvin Translation Society's *Tracts Relating to the
Reformation* (Edinburgh, 1844–1849) were converted from archive.org OCR of
the printed volumes — mechanical noise has been removed by script, but
occasional character errors may remain in those texts.

## Reply to Sadoleto (1539)

Calvin's celebrated answer to Cardinal Jacopo Sadoleto's letter inviting
Geneva back to Rome, written in six days from his Strasbourg exile.

- [[Library/protestant reformers/John Calvin/Treatises/Reply to Sadoleto/reply-to-sadoleto-index|Reply to Sadoleto — contents]]

## Short Treatise on the Lord's Supper (1541)

Written in French for ordinary believers, to settle troubled consciences
amid the eucharistic controversies; 60 short sections.

- [[Calvin Short Treatise on the Lord's Supper|Short Treatise on the Lord's Supper]]

## A Treatise on Relics (1543)

Calvin's satirical inventory of the relics venerated across Europe, with
the 19th-century translator's notes preserved as footnotes.

- [[Calvin Treatise on Relics|A Treatise on Relics]]

## The Necessity of Reforming the Church (1543)

Calvin's defence of the Reformation, presented in the name of all who
"wish Christ to reign" to the Imperial Diet at Spires (1544).

- [[Calvin Necessity of Reforming the Church|The Necessity of Reforming the Church]]

## Catechism of the Church of Geneva (1545)

A form of instruction for children in the doctrine of Christ, as a
Master–Scholar dialogue in five parts.

- [[Library/protestant reformers/John Calvin/Treatises/Catechism of the Church of Geneva/catechism-of-geneva-index|Catechism of the Church of Geneva — contents]]

## On the Christian Life (1559)

The "Golden Booklet of the True Christian Life" — Institutes III.6–10,
long circulated as a standalone devotional treatise.

- [[Library/protestant reformers/John Calvin/Treatises/On the Christian Life/on-the-christian-life-index|On the Christian Life — contents]]

## Of Prayer (1559)

CCEL also circulates *Of Prayer — A Perpetual Exercise of Faith* as a
standalone treatise; it is verbatim Institutes III.20, which is already in
this vault as [[Inst III.20 Of Prayer—A Perpetual Exercise of Faith|Institutes III.20]],
so it is not duplicated here.
