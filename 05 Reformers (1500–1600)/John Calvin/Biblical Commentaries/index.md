# Biblical Commentaries

John Calvin's commentaries on Scripture, in the 45-volume Calvin Translation Society edition (Edinburgh, 1844–1856), converted from CCEL ThML sources (calcom01–45). Calvin wrote commentaries or lectures on most of the Bible; each book below links to its own index. One note per Bible chapter, except the two harmonies noted below, which follow their sources' own topical structure.

## Deviations from the one-note-per-chapter format

- **Harmony of the Law** (calcom03–06): Calvin arranged Exodus–Deuteronomy topically (a 'History' narrative plus the Law by commandment), so its folder has one note per source division rather than per Bible chapter.
- **Harmony of the Evangelists** (calcom31–33): Matthew, Mark, and Luke are combined into one harmonized sequence; one note per harmonized passage division.
- **Ezekiel** stops at chapter 20 — Calvin died before completing the lectures.
- **Psalm 119** is split across calcom11/calcom12 in the source; it is merged into a single note here.
- The dedication in calcom45 covers the whole Catholic Epistles volume; it is filed under James (first of those books in canonical order).

## Books Calvin did not cover

Judges, Ruth, 1 Samuel, 2 Samuel, 1 Kings, 2 Kings, 1 Chronicles, 2 Chronicles, Ezra, Nehemiah, Esther, Job, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Solomon, 2 John, 3 John, Revelation.

(Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, and Deuteronomy are covered only via the Harmony of the Law; Matthew, Mark, and Luke only via the Harmony of the Evangelists; Ezekiel only through chapter 20.)
