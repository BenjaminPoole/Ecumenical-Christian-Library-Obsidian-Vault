# Harmony of the Evangelists

Calvin's three-volume commentary on a harmony of Matthew, Mark, and Luke (CCEL calcom31–33), translated by William Pringle for the Calvin Translation Society. **Deviation from the per-Bible-chapter format:** Calvin combined the three Synoptic Gospels into a single harmonized narrative, so no per-chapter notes exist for Matthew, Mark, or Luke; this folder follows the source's own structure — one note per harmonized passage division, numbered continuously across the three volumes. Each note's title lists the Gospel passage(s) treated together.
