# John Calvin (1509–1564)

French reformer and theologian of Geneva, whose *Institutes*, biblical
commentaries, and tracts shaped Reformed Protestantism. This collection
gathers his major works in the classic 19th-century English translations
of the Calvin Translation Society, converted from CCEL ThML transcriptions
and (for some treatises) archive.org scans of the printed volumes.

## Collections

- [[Library/protestant reformers/John Calvin/Institutes of the Christian Religion/institutes-index|Institutes of the Christian Religion]] — Calvin's systematic theology in four books (1559 final edition, Beveridge translation); 80 chapters plus prefatory material.
- [[Library/protestant reformers/John Calvin/Biblical Commentaries/commentaries-index|Biblical Commentaries]] — the 45-volume Calvin Translation Society edition: commentaries or lectures on most of the Bible, one note per chapter, plus the Harmony of the Law and the Harmony of the Evangelists.
- [[Library/protestant reformers/John Calvin/Treatises/treatises-index|Treatises]] — shorter works: the Reply to Sadoleto, Short Treatise on the Lord's Supper, Treatise on Relics, The Necessity of Reforming the Church, the Geneva Catechism, and On the Christian Life.
