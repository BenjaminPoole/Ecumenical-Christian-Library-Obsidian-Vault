# The History of the Reformation in Scotland, Book Fifth

The continuation of Knox's History covering 1564-1567, first printed in 1644 and traditionally ascribed to Knox's papers though not finished by his own hand. Laing's edition (Works, vol. 2), original spelling; begins with Laing's introductory notice. Books One to Four are in the modern-spelling CCEL edition in this collection.
