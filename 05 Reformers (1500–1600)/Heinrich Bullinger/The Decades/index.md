# The Decades

Bullinger's fifty sermons in five decades - his systematic exposition of Christian doctrine, which Elizabethan convocation ordered the lesser clergy to study. Parker Society edition (Cambridge, 1849-52), from the Elizabethan translation by 'H. I.', edited by Thomas Harding. Sermon titles follow the printed captions.
