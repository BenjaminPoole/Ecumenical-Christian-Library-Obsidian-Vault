# John Wycliffe (c. 1328–1384)

English scholastic philosopher and theologian at Oxford, called 'the Morning Star of the Reformation'. He attacked papal authority, transubstantiation and clerical wealth, and inspired the first complete English Bible. His followers were the Lollards.
