# The Last Age of the Church

A Middle English apocalyptic tract of 1356, printed by J. H. Todd in 1840 as Wycliffe's - the attribution is doubted by later scholarship, but it remains the first 'Wycliffite' text ever printed. Left in its original Middle English; Todd's editorial notes follow as a second note.
