# Jan Hus (c. 1372–1415)

Czech priest and rector of Prague University who took up Wycliffe's teaching, preached against indulgences and clerical corruption, and was burned at the Council of Constance in 1415 despite a promise of safe conduct. His death provoked the Hussite wars.
