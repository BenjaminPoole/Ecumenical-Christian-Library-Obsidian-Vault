# 45. Letter XLIV. To John of Chlum

LETTER XLIV. TO JOHN OF CHLUM

(February 1415)

Don't send his letters on ; Sigismund ought to judge him ; Procure that at his trial he be near him ; No proctor save Jesus ; The charges against him ; The matter of the safeconduct ; The King ought not to allow him to be thrust back into prison 179-81
