# The Latin Works and Correspondence of Huldreich Zwingli

The three-volume American edition of Zwingli's Latin works and correspondence in English translation, the widest public-domain collection of Zwingli in English. Each volume prints discrete, individually titled works (numbered I onward in the printed tables of contents, or given their own headings), which are one note per work here. Vol. 1 (1912, ed. Jackson): Myconius's original life of Zwingli, then fourteen items from *The Ox* fable of 1510 through the *Archeteles* of 1522. Vol. 2 (1922, ed. Hinke): six items from the 1526 declaration on original sin through the 1531 short exposition of the Christian faith addressed to Francis I of France. Vol. 3 (1929, ed. Heller): Zwingli's major systematic work, the *Commentary on True and False Religion* (1525), split by its own printed sections (Introduction, Address to Francis I, then the roughly thirty numbered topical sections from ‘God’ through the Epilogue), followed by the *Reply to Jerome Emser* (1524), similarly split by its sections. For the Zurich disputation documents in a cleaner text, see the Selected Works in this collection.
