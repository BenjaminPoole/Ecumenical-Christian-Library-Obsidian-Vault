# The Translation of Certain Psalms into English Verse

*Francis Bacon* — EEBO-TCP A01612, keyed from the 1625 edition.

Seven psalms put into English verse in the last year of Bacon's life, during a long illness, and dedicated to George Herbert — *in respect of our ancient and private acquaintance, and in respect of your gifts, being not only a great wit, but a great poet.* The only verse Bacon published.
