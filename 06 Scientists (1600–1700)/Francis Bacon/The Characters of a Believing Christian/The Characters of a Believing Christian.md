# The Characters of a Believing Christian

*Francis Bacon* — EEBO-TCP A28370, keyed from *The Remaines of the Right Honorable Francis, Lord Verulam* (1648).

Three short religious pieces printed together at the end of Bacon's *Remaines*: the *Characters*, which sets out the Christian life as a string of paradoxes; a second and fuller recension of the *Confession of the Faith*; and the prayer Bacon wrote after his fall. The *Characters* has since been claimed for Herbert Palmer, whose *Memorials of Godlinesse* printed it in 1645; it is given here as the *Remaines* gives it.
