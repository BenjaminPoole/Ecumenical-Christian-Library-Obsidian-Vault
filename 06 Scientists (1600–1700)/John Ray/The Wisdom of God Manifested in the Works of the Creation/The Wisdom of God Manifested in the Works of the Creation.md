# The Wisdom of God Manifested in the Works of the Creation

*John Ray* — EEBO-TCP A58185, keyed from the 1691 first edition.

The book that made natural theology an English habit of mind, grown out of Ray's divinity lectures in the chapel of Trinity College, Cambridge. It clears the Epicurean and Cartesian accounts of the world out of the way and then goes through the creation in order — the heavens, the elements, meteors, minerals, plants, and at greatest length animals and the human body — reading contrivance off each in turn. Paley's *Natural Theology* is its grandchild, and Darwin read it.
