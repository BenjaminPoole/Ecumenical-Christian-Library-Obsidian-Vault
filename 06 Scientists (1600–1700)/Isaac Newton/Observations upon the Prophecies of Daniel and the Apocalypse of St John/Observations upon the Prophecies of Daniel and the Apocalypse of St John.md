# Observations upon the Prophecies of Daniel and the Apocalypse of St John

*Isaac Newton* — from the first edition, London 1733, by way of [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/16878).

Newton spent more of his life on prophecy and chronology than on optics, and left this unpublished at his death; his nephew Benjamin Smith saw it through the press in 1733. It is not a work of enthusiasm but of scholarship in Newton's own manner — the prophetic images treated as a language with fixed meanings, the text of Daniel dated from the compilers of the Old Testament canon, the seventy weeks computed, and the eleventh horn of the fourth beast identified with the Church of Rome. The second part does the same for the Apocalypse, and argues that it was written before the destruction of Jerusalem.
