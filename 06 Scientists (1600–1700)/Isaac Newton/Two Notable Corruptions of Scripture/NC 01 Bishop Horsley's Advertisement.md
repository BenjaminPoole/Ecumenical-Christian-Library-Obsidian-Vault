# Bishop Horsley's Advertisement

A very imperfect copy of this Tract, wanting both the beginning and the end, and erroneous in many places, was published at London in the year 1754, under the title of Two Letters from Sir Isaac Newton to Mr. Le Clerc. But in the Author’s MS. the whole is one continued discourse; which, although it is conceived in the epistolary form, is not addressed to any particular person.
