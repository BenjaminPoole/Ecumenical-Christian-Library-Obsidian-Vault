# The Martyrdom of Theodora and of Didymus

*Robert Boyle* — EEBO-TCP A28990, keyed from the 1687 edition.

Written in Boyle's early twenties and published forty years later at the urging of friends: the martyrdom under Diocletian of Theodora of Antioch, sentenced to a brothel for refusing to sacrifice, and of the Christian soldier Didymus who changed clothes with her to get her out and died in her place. Boyle tells it as a romance rather than a chronicle, and it is the only thing of the kind he wrote. Handel set Thomas Morell's libretto from it as *Theodora* in 1750.
