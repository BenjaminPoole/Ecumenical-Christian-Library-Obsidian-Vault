# A Discourse of Things Above Reason

*Robert Boyle* — EEBO-TCP A28958, keyed from the 1681 edition.

A dialogue between four speakers on whether a philosopher may admit that some things are above reason, and on the difference between a doctrine reason cannot reach and one it can refute. The distinction is Boyle's standing answer to both the sceptics and the dogmatists, and the *Advices* printed with it are six rules for keeping it.
