# Postscript to the Preface

To give an Account of the Prolixity, that some might otherwise censure, of the foregoing *PREFACE*, I must Advertise the Reader, That 'tis of an ancient Date, and that the *First Part* of the Treatise, that it belongs to, was already written, and 'twas then designed, that the *Second Part* should accompany it to the Press: On which score 'twas presumed, that, as the Particulars that make up the Preamble would not appear Superfluous, in regard of the Variety of Subjects to be Treated of; So, its Length would scarce be found Disproportionate to the Bulk of the Whole designed Book.
