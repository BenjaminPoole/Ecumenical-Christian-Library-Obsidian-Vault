# The Reconcileableness of Reason and Religion

*Robert Boyle* — EEBO-TCP A29027, keyed from the 1675 edition.

Published under the initials T.E., *a lay-man*. Boyle takes the objection that the articles of faith affront reason and separates it into parts: what reason is, what it can settle, where its own principles run out, and what follows for a revealed doctrine that lies past the end of them. *Some Physico-Theological Considerations about the Possibility of the Resurrection* is annexed.
