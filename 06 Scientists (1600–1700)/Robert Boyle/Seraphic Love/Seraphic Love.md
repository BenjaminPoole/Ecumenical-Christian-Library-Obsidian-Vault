# Seraphic Love

*Robert Boyle* — EEBO-TCP A29033, keyed from the 1659 edition, printed as *Some Motives and Incentives to the Love of God*.

Boyle's first published book, written at twenty-one as a letter to a friend disappointed in love, and the work he was best known for in his own lifetime — it went through six editions. Its argument is that the love of God answers everything the love of a creature promises and fails to give, and it is written throughout in the heightened style Boyle afterwards gave up.
