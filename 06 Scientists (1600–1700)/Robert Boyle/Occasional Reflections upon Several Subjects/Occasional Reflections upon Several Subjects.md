# Occasional Reflections upon Several Subjects

*Robert Boyle* — EEBO-TCP A29010, keyed from the 1665 edition.

Meditations made out of whatever happened to be in front of him, with the discourse defending the practice printed before them. The six sections are of different kinds: the second is written to his sister during a fit of the ague, and the fourth is a day's fishing with *Eusebius* and *Lindamor*, in which every accident of the river is turned to account. Swift parodied the whole in *A Meditation upon a Broomstick*, which has kept the book better known by its mockery than by itself.
