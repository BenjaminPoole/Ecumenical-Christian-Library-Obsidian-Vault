# A Discourse Concerning the Beauty of Providence

*John Wilkins* — EEBO-TCP A66025, keyed from the 1649 edition.

Printed in the year of the king's execution and offered as *very seasonable to quiet and support the heart in these times of public confusion*. Wilkins's subject is the providence that is only beautiful when the whole of it is seen, and the temper proper to those who are inside a piece of history and cannot see it.
