# Sermons Preached Before the King at White-Hall

*John Wilkins* — EEBO-TCP A66060, keyed from the 1677 edition.

Three sermons Wilkins preached before Charles II in the last years of his life, on the length of days in Wisdom's right hand, on the whole duty of man, and on the judgement of every secret thing. The volume printed them with the *Beauty of Providence*, which is given in the vault as its own work.
