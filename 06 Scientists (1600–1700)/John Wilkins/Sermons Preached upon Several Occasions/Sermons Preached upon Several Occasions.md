# Sermons Preached upon Several Occasions

*John Wilkins* — EEBO-TCP A66062, keyed from the 1682 edition.

Fifteen sermons found among Wilkins's papers and published ten years after his death: on the recompence of reward, on the kingdom of God as righteousness and peace, on adorning the doctrine of God, on wisdom and its companions, on moderation, and two on leaving vengeance to God. They are the preaching the *Ecclesiastes* recommends, plain and argued.
