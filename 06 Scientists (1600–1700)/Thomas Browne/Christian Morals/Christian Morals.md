# Christian Morals

*Thomas Browne* — from *The Works of Sir Thomas Browne* volume 3, at [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/39962).

Browne's last work, found among his papers and printed in 1716 by John Jeffery, Archdeacon of Norwich. A book of counsel in three parts, written in the late manner Johnson admired and imitated: *Tread softly and circumspectly in this funambulatory track and narrow path of goodness.*
