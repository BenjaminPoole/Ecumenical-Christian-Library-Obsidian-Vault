# Charles Haddon Spurgeon

Charles Haddon Spurgeon (1834–1892), the “Prince of Preachers,” was the Reformed Baptist pastor of the New Park Street Chapel and then the Metropolitan Tabernacle in London for thirty-eight years. He preached to thousands weekly, founded a pastors’ college and an orphanage, and left one of the largest bodies of published work of any Christian author. All texts here are public domain — the sermons and books from the [Christian Classics Ethereal Library](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/spurgeon), the Treasury of David from [The Spurgeon Archive](https://archive.spurgeon.org/treasury/treasury.php).

> *“A Bible that’s falling apart usually belongs to someone who isn’t.”*
