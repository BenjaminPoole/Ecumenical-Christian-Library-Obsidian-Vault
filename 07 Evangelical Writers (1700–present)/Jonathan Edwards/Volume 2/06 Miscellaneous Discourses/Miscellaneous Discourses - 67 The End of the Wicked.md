# THE END OF THE WICKED

### CONTEMPLATED

## BY THE RIGHTEOUS:

### OR,

## THE TORMENTS OF THE WICKED IN HELL, NO OCCASION OF GRIEF TO THE SAINTS IN HEAVEN.

Rev. xviii. 20.

Rejoice over her, thou heaven, and ye holy apostles and prophets; for God hath avenged you an her. [^190]

[^190]: The substance of two posthumous discourses, dated March, 1773.
