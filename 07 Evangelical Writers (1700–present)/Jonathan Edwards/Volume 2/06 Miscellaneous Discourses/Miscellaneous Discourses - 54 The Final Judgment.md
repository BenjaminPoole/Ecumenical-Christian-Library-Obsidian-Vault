# THE FINAL JUDGMENT:

### OR,

## THE WORLD JUDGED RIGHTEOUSLY BY JESUS CHRIST.

ACTS xvii. 31.

Because he hath appointed a day, in the which he will judge the world in righteousness by that man whom he hath ordained.
