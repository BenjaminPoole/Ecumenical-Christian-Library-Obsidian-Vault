# The Treasury of David

Spurgeon’s monumental commentary on the Psalms — twenty years in the writing (1865–1885). For each Psalm: an **Exposition**, **Explanatory Notes and Quaint Sayings** gathered from many authors, and **Hints to the Village Preacher**. Text from [The Spurgeon Archive](https://archive.spurgeon.org/treasury/treasury.php).
