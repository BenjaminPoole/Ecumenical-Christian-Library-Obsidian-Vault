# Sermons on Proverbs

Spurgeon's sermons on texts from the book of Proverbs, gathered as a volume by the [Christian Classics Ethereal Library](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/spurgeon). The sermons themselves were preached across his ministry and published in the weekly pulpit series.
