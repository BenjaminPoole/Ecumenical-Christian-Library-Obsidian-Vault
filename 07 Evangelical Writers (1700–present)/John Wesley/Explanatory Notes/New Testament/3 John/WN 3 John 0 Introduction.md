# 3 John — Introduction

THE third epistle has likewise three parts:

- I. The inscription, 1, 2
- II. The commendation of Caius, 3-8 With a caution against Diotrephes 9-11 And a recommendation of Demetrius, 12
- III. The conclusion, 13-15
