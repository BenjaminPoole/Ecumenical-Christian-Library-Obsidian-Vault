# Expository Thoughts on the Gospels

Ryle’s magnum opus (1856–1865): a plain, practical, verse-by-verse exposition of the four Gospels, originally issued in seven volumes “for family and private use”.

Text from the Grace Gems edition, whose wording is lightly modernised (e.g. “Holy Spirit” for “Holy Ghost”); Ryle’s original footnotes are not included in that edition.
